Attic
Privacy GitHub

A music player for the MP3s already on your phone.

No account, no streaming, no ads. It plays in airplane mode.

Coming to Google Play Download the APK Source on GitHub

Two styles, one app

Pick one in Settings, under Appearance. Both come in light and dark, and you can switch at any time.

What it does

Plays your library

  • Album, artist and playlist screens, tinted from the cover art
  • A queue you drag to reorder and swipe to remove from
  • Favourites and your own playlists, stored on the phone

Finds what to play next, offline

  • Start radio from any song, and Attic keeps ten similar songs in the queue
  • Nine instant mixes on Home, changing daily
  • Learns from skips, finishes and likes in a listening log kept on the phone

Keeps playing outside the app

  • Notification and lock-screen controls, including shuffle and like
  • Bluetooth and headset media buttons
  • A resizable home-screen widget with five recent albums

Shows lyrics, then keeps them

  • Synced lyrics highlight the current line; tap a line to jump to it
  • Download from LRCLIB for one song or the whole library, then read them offline

Cleans up messy tags

  • "Artist - Song (Lyrics)" by an uploader shows as Song by Artist
  • Three spellings of one artist become one artist page
  • Nothing is written back to your files

Recap

  • Your top songs, artists and listening clock for the week, month, year or all time
  • Cards you can save or share as images

Privacy

Attic has no account, analytics or ads, and playback and song analysis never touch the network.

The one exception is lyrics: when you download them, Attic sends the song's title, artist, album and length to lrclib.net. Your music files are never uploaded.

Install

Attic needs Android 13 or newer and a 64-bit ARM phone.

  1. Download the latest APK from GitHub Releases and open it on your phone.
  2. Put your MP3s in Music/Offline. Attic only looks in that folder.
  3. Open Attic and allow audio access and notifications.
