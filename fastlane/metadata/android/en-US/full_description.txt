Attic plays the songs already on your phone: MP3, M4A, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and WAV files. There is no account, no streaming and no ads, and it plays in airplane mode.

Copy your music onto the phone, in Music, Download or any other folder, and Attic builds albums and artists from it. Album and artist pages take their colour from the cover art.

PLAYS YOUR LIBRARY
- Home, Search and Library tabs, with album, artist and playlist screens
- A mini player and a full Now Playing screen, tinted from the cover art
- Shuffle, repeat, and a queue you can drag to reorder and swipe to remove from
- Favourites and your own playlists, stored on the device
- Dark and light themes, or match the phone
- Two styles to pick from in Settings: Expressive and Comfort

FINDS WHAT TO PLAY NEXT, OFFLINE
- Start radio from any song: Attic keeps ten similar songs in the queue, where you can reorder or remove them as usual
- Instant mixes on Home: nine tiles of your favourites and unplayed songs that sound like them, changing daily
- Stays in the language you're listening to, with about one song in ten from another language when the match is strong
- Plays one version of a song when it exists in more than one language
- Learns from skips, finishes and likes in a listening log kept on the phone

Radio works by analysing each song's sound on the phone. Nothing is uploaded. Analysis runs in the background while the phone is charging.

KEEPS PLAYING OUTSIDE THE APP
- Background playback with notification and lock-screen controls, including shuffle and like
- Bluetooth and headset media buttons, and it pauses when another app takes audio focus
- A resizable home-screen widget with the current song, controls and five recent albums

SHOWS LYRICS, THEN KEEPS THEM
- Synced lyrics highlight the current line, and tapping a line jumps to it
- Download lyrics for one song or the whole library from LRCLIB, then use them offline

CLEANS UP MESSY TAGS
Downloaded files often have no album tag and an uploader's name where the artist should be. Attic works out what the tags meant: "Artist - Song (Lyrics)" shows as Song by Artist, a video ID in the file name is dropped, and three spellings of one artist become one artist page. Nothing is written back to your files.

RECAP
See your top songs, artists and listening clock for the week, month, year or all time, and share any of the cards as an image.

PRIVACY
Attic has no account, analytics or ads, and playback and song analysis never touch the network. The one exception is lyrics: when you download them, Attic sends the song's title, artist, album and length to lrclib.net. Your music files are never uploaded.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Attic finds songs in any folder on the phone or its SD card, and leaves out ringtones, recordings, podcasts, audiobooks and voice notes
- It needs Android 13 or newer and a 64-bit ARM phone
- It was built for one phone and its library of 740 MP3 and M4A songs, and the release build has been tested on that phone
