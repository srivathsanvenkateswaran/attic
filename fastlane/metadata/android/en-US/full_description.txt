Attic plays the songs already on your phone: MP3, M4A, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and WAV files. There is no account, no streaming and no ads, and it plays in airplane mode.

Copy your music into any folder on the phone and Attic builds albums and artists from it.

PLAYS YOUR LIBRARY
- Home, Search and Library tabs, with album, artist and playlist screens
- A mini player and a full Now Playing screen, tinted from the cover art
- Shuffle, repeat, and a queue you can drag to reorder and swipe to remove from
- Swipe any song row to add it to the queue
- Favourites and your own playlists, stored on the device
- Dark and light themes, or match the phone
- Two styles to pick from in Settings: Expressive and Comfort
- Tablet layouts: a side rail, and two panes when the tablet is sideways

FINDS WHAT TO PLAY NEXT, OFFLINE
- Start radio from any song: Attic keeps ten similar songs queued
- Mixes on Home, new each day: nine instant mixes from your favourites, and mixes of the songs you play, grouped by how they sound
- Mood mixes: a mix a day for Chill, Focus, Party and four more moods, from how your songs sound and the moods, genres and languages you pick. Each has its own radio
- On repeat, Haven't heard in a while and Newly added shelves on Home
- Smart shuffle for playlists and Favourites, adding similar songs from the rest of your library
- Genres from your files' tags or from analysis, and radio from any genre
- Stays in the language you're listening to, with about one song in ten from another language when the match is strong
- Plays one version of a song that exists in more than one language
- Learns from skips, finishes and likes in a listening log kept on the phone

Radio, mixes, moods and genres come from analysing each song's sound on the phone while it charges. Nothing is uploaded.

PLAYBACK
- A ten-band equaliser with eleven presets and your own. A song or an album can keep its own setting, which comes back whenever it plays
- It runs inside Attic, so it sounds the same on every phone. Boost presets play quieter rather than distort
- Smart transitions start the next song where this one winds down, skip silence and line up beats when tempos are close. Or a plain 2 to 12 s crossfade
- Volume levelling, from ReplayGain tags or the loudness measured during analysis

KEEPS PLAYING OUTSIDE THE APP
- Background playback with notification and lock-screen controls, including shuffle and like
- Bluetooth and headset buttons, and a pause when another app takes audio focus
- A resizable home-screen widget with the current song, controls and five recent albums
- Supports Android Auto: browse your library, search, and ask for music by voice

SHOWS LYRICS, THEN KEEPS THEM
- Synced lyrics highlight the current line; tap a line to jump to it
- Download lyrics for one song or the whole library from LRCLIB, then use them offline

CLEANS UP MESSY TAGS
Downloaded files often have no album tag and an uploader's name where the artist should be. Attic works out what the tags meant: "Artist - Song (Lyrics)" shows as Song by Artist, and three spellings of one artist become one artist page. Nothing is written back to your files.

RECAP
See your top songs, artists and listening clock for the week, month, year or all time, and share any card as an image.

PRIVACY
Attic has no account, analytics or ads, and playback and song analysis never touch the network. The one exception is lyrics: when you download them, Attic sends the song's title, artist, album and length to lrclib.net. Your music files are never uploaded.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Attic finds songs in any folder on the phone or its SD card, and leaves out ringtones, recordings, podcasts, audiobooks and voice notes
- It needs Android 13 or newer and a 64-bit ARM phone
- Tested on one phone with 740 songs, including at a tablet's screen size
