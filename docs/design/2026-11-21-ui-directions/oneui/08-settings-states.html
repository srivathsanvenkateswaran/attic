Settings and system states

2026-11-21 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 08

Settings is the screen a Galaxy owner has seen most, so it is built exactly to pattern: a large title alone in the upper third, section labels on the ground, one card per section, radios leading their rows, a pill switch trailing its row with the explanation under the label. Paragraphs, progress and the buttons that belong to them sit inside the card they describe.

The states on this sheet are the ones with nothing to show: before permission, during the first scan, an empty library, an item that has been removed. Each uses the kit's empty block and says what to do next. The permission screen keeps its words and moves its one button to the bottom edge.

What was kept

  • Sections "Appearance", "Lyrics", "Song radio", "About", in that order, reached from the settings button on each tab root, with the mini player and tab bar still on screen.
  • "Match phone", "Dark", "Light", applied on tap.
  • Lyrics: "{saved} of {total} songs have lyrics saved on this phone. Saved lyrics work offline.", the lrclib.net sentence, "Download lyrics for all songs", the running state with "Checked {done} of {total}, found {found}" and no button, and both failure sentences.
  • Song radio: "Autoplay" with its sentence and a switch, the on-device sentence, the bar that is always visible, the three status lines, and "Analyse now without a charger" only when analysis is idle and songs remain.
  • About: the sentence with the song count.
  • Permission: the title, both body sentences, "Allow access" and "Open app permissions".
  • Loading: a spinner and nothing else.
  • "No songs in Music/Offline" and "{This album | This artist | This playlist} is gone" with their sentences.

Open questions

  • Back arrow under the title. One UI puts the back arrow in the bar at the foot of the expanded title, which is where it lands when the title collapses. On a screen this short of chrome it can look detached. The alternative is a bar at the very top with the arrow, and a shorter title area.
  • "Back" on a missing item. A button that does what the arrow does. It is there for reach. Drop it if a duplicate control is worse than a stretch.
  • Empty Home without sub-tabs. Frame D2 hides "All / Albums / Artists / Folders" when the library is empty. If the app keeps them visible today, keeping them is one line.
  • Theme previews. One UI's own display settings show a small picture of each theme above its radio. Three plain rows were kept because the kit defines radios as rows and the change is instant on tap.
  • Failure line colour. The two lyrics failure sentences get a red glyph and stay in ink. Fully red text was avoided because "6 songs couldn't be checked" is not an error the listener caused.
  • Light permission screen. A fresh install defaults to "Dark", so frame F in light only happens after a theme change and a revoked permission. It is drawn in light to check the layout in both themes.
