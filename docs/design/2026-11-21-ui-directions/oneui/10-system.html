Outside the app

2026-11-21 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 10

The four places Attic shows up without being open: the home-screen widget, the media controls in the notification panel and on the lock screen, the notification shown while songs are analysed, and the launcher icon. Attic draws the widget and the icon. The system draws the other two from what Attic hands it, so those frames are in grey system chrome, and frame F lists exactly what the app supplies.

The sheet settles the widget's surface (a card washed with the cover, where today it is a block of the cover's colour), the favourite glyph in the media controls, and the icon's ground, colours and themed form. The mark itself is a stand-in: the current house outline, recoloured, until the redesign in logo/ is chosen.

What was kept

  • One widget in three layouts chosen by its size: default 4 × 2, minimum 110 × 50dp, resizable both ways. The picker text, "Your song and controls, with your recent albums. Resize it for a smaller player."
  • Large: cover, title, artist, previous, play or pause, next, the mark in the corner, five album shortcuts. Wide: cover, title, artist, three buttons. Small: cover and play or pause.
  • The five albums are the most recently played, topped up with the biggest; a tap opens that album. A tap anywhere else that is not a button opens the app.
  • "Attic" and "Tap to open" when nothing is queued. The last song is kept across a reboot and shown paused (frame A2 is that state).
  • Button descriptions "Play or pause", "Next", "Previous", "Open album".
  • Media controls: title, artist, album, artwork read from the file, the white mark, previous, play or pause, next, seek, shuffle and favourite. A tap opens the app. Nothing is posted until playback starts.
  • Analysis notification: ongoing and silent on "Song analysis", the equaliser icon, "Analysing your songs", "{done} of {total}", a determinate bar, "Stop". A tap opens the app.
  • The launcher icon keeps a monochrome layer.

What the direction cannot reach

The media card and the analysis notification are system templates. Their layout, type, corner radii, the thin seek line and progress bar, the artwork's corners and the output chip come from One UI and change between its versions; frames B, B2 and C draw one plausible version to show where Attic's pieces land. The widget has no progress fill because it has no progress today, and a home-screen widget cannot tick once a second without waking the app.

Kit additions in this sheet

In the sheet's <style>, ready to fold into kit.css: the widget .wg in three sizes and the launcher icon .aicon. The home screen, panel, lock screen, media card and notification classes are system stand-ins for this sheet only and should stay out of the kit.

Open questions

  • The icon's ground. Proposed: Lamplight ground, Soot mark. It reads on dark and light wallpapers and is the same lockup as the stamp on a Recap card. The alternative in frame D, Night ground with a Lamplight mark, is calmer and nearer today's icon in structure. Both use the same two colours.
  • A widget that follows the theme. Today the widget is the cover's colour in both themes. Here it is a dark washed card in dark and a pale one in light. If it should stay dark on every home screen, it takes the dark tokens only.
  • Type in the widget. It is drawn in Onest. Today the widget uses the system font. If a bundled face in a widget layout turns out to be unreliable on some launchers, the system font is the fallback and the layout holds.
  • No title at the small size. As today, 2 × 2 is the cover and one button. The card leaves no room for a title without shrinking the cover to about half.
  • Which One UI. The panel is drawn with the app icon on each notification, as One UI 7 does. On One UI 6 the same notification shows the small equaliser icon in a circle instead.
