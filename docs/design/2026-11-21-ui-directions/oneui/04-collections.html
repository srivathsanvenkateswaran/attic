What was kept

  • Play or pause for the collection, shuffle with an on state, add the whole collection to a playlist. All three are off for an empty collection.
  • Header facts: "Album" or "Single" with the year, the artist link, "{n} songs, {duration}", "Playlist from your Music/Offline folder".
  • The album's closing line "{n} songs • {duration}", under the card.
  • Artist: five ranked songs, then albums newest first, then the remaining songs by title. Playing any song queues the artist's whole list.
  • Playlist: add songs, reorder by dragging a handle, rename and delete from "Playlist options", "Remove from this playlist" in a row's menu. Reorder is off with fewer than two songs.
  • Favourites newest first. Folders read-only.
  • Tap a row to play in context; long-press or the more button for the track menu. In reorder mode a tap does nothing and long-press still opens the menu.
  • The title collapses into the bar on scroll.
  • Nothing from the "not present today" list: no sort on a collection, no favourite for an album or artist.

Checked against today's album screen

Frame A next to screenshots/album.png: the tinted wash, the black disc behind the back arrow, the left-aligned title, the round artist thumbnail, the five-part control row and the green circle are all gone, and the rows have gained a container, numbers and lengths. The vertical order (cover, name, artist, type and year, actions, tracks) and the wording "Album • 2026" carry over, and are meant to.

Open questions

  • Playlist tools in the card. The kit's note has a playlist add two more toggles to the button row, five controls across. This sheet moves them into the card as words instead. It costs 56dp of list at rest and makes the button row identical on every collection. If the owner prefers five across, the kit already draws it.
  • Cover at 176dp. The direction sheet shows 196dp. At 196 the album's third track, the one playing in the fixture, ends in the fade above the dock, and a playlist shows two songs. 176 keeps the third row clear on an album and brings a third into view on a playlist.
  • Track numbers and lengths. Today's rows show neither. The files carry both, so nothing new is stored, but it is more on each album row than the owner sees now. Playlist rows are unchanged: cover, title, artist.
  • Empty-state wording. Both messages point at a plus that is no longer there: on a song it is now a heart, and on a playlist it is a button reading "Add songs". The rewritten sentences are outside the naming table.
  • "Album • 2026". The bullet is on the list of Spotify cues and is kept here because the string is not in the naming table. "Album, 2026" would match "14 songs, 1 hr 13 min".
  • Ranks on "Top songs". The order behind them is most recently played, as today. A bold rank suggests a count that does not exist; the rows would work without it.
  • Reorder mode hides the mini player. Music keeps playing, and "Done" brings the dock back. One UI hides bottom navigation in every edit mode, but it is the one place in the app where the mini player is out of reach.
