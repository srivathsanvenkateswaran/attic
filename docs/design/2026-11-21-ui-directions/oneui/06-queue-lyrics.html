Queue and lyrics

2026-11-21 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 06

Both screens open from Now Playing, so both stand on its ground: the black that leans toward the cover, with the cover's colour spent on what is playing. In the Queue that is the current song's card, filled as far as the song has played. In Lyrics it is the tonal bed under the line being sung.

The sheet settles where the one destructive action goes ("Clear" comes down from the top corner to the bottom edge), how radio shows in a queue, and what replaces the flat colour field with white and dark lines that full-screen lyrics use today.

What was kept

  • Queue: "Now playing", "Up next, {n} songs" and "Up next, 1 song", "Nothing up next", "Clear" removing everything after the current song.
  • Tap a row to jump to it, drag the handle to reorder, swipe a row left or right to remove it with "Remove" on the strip behind, long-press for the track menu. The kebab on the current song opens the same menu.
  • No history: songs already played are not listed. Shuffle shows as the real order.
  • Radio: the queue refills to ten, swiping a song out steers it, "Clear" stops it.
  • Lyrics: synced lines that follow playback and scroll so the current line sits about a third of the way down, tap a line to seek to it, a note glyph for a blank timed line, plain lyrics as one scrolling block, "Download lyrics" when there is nothing saved, play or pause and nothing else in the footer.
  • Every card state and its sentence: "Download once and they stay on this phone.", "This song is instrumental.", "No lyrics were found for this song.", "Download lyrics", and "Open lyrics" (renamed from "Show lyrics").
  • The snackbars "No lyrics found for {title}" and "Connect to the internet to download lyrics". Over the Queue the toast sits above "Clear".

Open questions

  • "Clear" within reach, with no undo. It removes the whole queue in one tap and shows no message, as today. At the top corner it was hard to hit by accident. If the bottom position stays, it may want the bottom dialog from sheet 07 in front of it.
  • New words. "Radio keeps ten lined up" and "Play next and Add to queue, in any song's menu, line songs up here." are not in the app. The context at the end of the "Now playing" label is an existing string in a new place.
  • Title at the bottom of Lyrics. Today the song title is at the top. Here the top says only "Lyrics" and the title rides with play or pause. If the title should stay high, the bar takes it as a two-line title and the bottom row keeps the cover, the fill and the button.
  • Lyrics in light. Today the lyrics screen is always dark. Frame G assumes it follows the theme like Now Playing does in sheet 05. One decision covers both.
  • Per-line fill. The bed could fill from the left while its line is sung, using the gap to the next line's timestamp. It was left out to keep the screen still; it would be the fourth use of the fill.
  • Play button under 48dp. The button in the bottom row is drawn 44dp. Its touch target is the full height of the row.
