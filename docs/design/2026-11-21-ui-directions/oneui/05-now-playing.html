Now Playing

2026-11-21 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 05

The player is split the way One UI splits a screen. What you look at is high: the cover, the title, the line being sung. What you touch is low, gathered in one card, the deck. The heart moves off the title row and into the deck with the other toggles, because it is a control and the title is not.

The sheet settles the seek bar and what album-art colour is spent on. The seek bar is a thick track in the style of a quick-panel slider, and its fill carries the elapsed time. The cover's colour goes to whatever shows progress or state: the seek fill, the play button, a lit toggle, the played part of the mini player. Surfaces only lean toward it.

What was kept

  • Every control: close, track menu, seek by tap or drag, shuffle, previous, play or pause, next, repeat off / all / one, favourite, queue.
  • "Playing from" and every context string, including "Radio · {song title}".
  • Swipe the cover sideways to change song, pull down to close, tap the artist to open them.
  • The paused state on the cover (it is drawn at 86%).
  • The inline lyric line, the lyrics card, "About the artist", "Credits" with "Performed by", "Album", "Year", and the compact header after scrolling.
  • Elapsed and total time, both as m:ss. No time remaining.

How the colour is computed

.tinted on the phone replaces the accent tokens with mixes of the cover's --tint, so the seek fill, play button and lit toggles change with the song and no component rule mentions artwork. .wash moves the surface tokens: the ground is 44% of the cover's deep tone over black in dark, and 56% of its pale tone over plaster in light. In the app the three inputs come from the Palette swatch the code already extracts (ui/Art.kt:99-141), with one lighter and one darker step derived from it.

Open questions

  • Favourite in the deck. It is one row lower than today and no longer beside the title. If it should stay next to the title, the toggle row drops to three and spreads out.
  • Lit toggles in the cover's colour. Shuffle-on reads clearly on this cover. On a very dark cover the fill is lifted toward white by the same mix, which keeps it visible but makes it paler. Lamplight amber for lit toggles on every song is the alternative; it would be the only amber on this screen.
  • Light Now Playing. Today the player is always dark. Frame D assumes it follows the theme. Forcing dark is one class on the screen.
  • No peek of the lyrics card. The player page is exactly one screen, as today, so nothing shows that the page scrolls. Shortening the cover by 40dp would let the top of the lyrics card show.
