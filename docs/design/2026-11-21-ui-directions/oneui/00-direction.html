Direction: One UI

2026-11-21 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 00

Attic drawn with the grammar a Galaxy owner already knows from Settings, Gallery, My Files and the quick panel, and none of the Samsung apps' own screens. This sheet fixes the tokens and components the other ten sheets are built from. Everything on it is rendered from kit.css.

Borrowed from One UI. The split between a viewing area and an interaction area: a centred large title in an empty upper third that collapses to a compact bar, with controls gathered in the lower half. Grouped cards on a ground one step away, rows split by hairlines inset to the text, section labels outside the cards. Superellipses for thumbnails and icon containers. The component set: a floating capsule tab bar, bottom dialogs with two text buttons split by a divider, pop-over menus, pill switches, thick sliders, a bottom action bar, capsule toasts.

Attic's own. The accent is Lamplight, an amber, on neutrals that lean slightly warm; Samsung's blue is not used anywhere. One rule sorts the content: a collection is an object, a song is a row. The mini player and the tab bar are one floating card, the dock, so the bottom chrome obeys the grouped-card rule too. People get a rounder superellipse than records. The typeface is Onest. The mark is the house with the round window, in Lamplight, with its corners softened to match the icons.

The signature is the fill. Progress is never a thin line. It is the played part of a thick shape, filled with the colour of the cover: the seek bar with the time inside it, the mini player filling from the left, the analysis bar in Settings. Album-art colour is spent on progress and on-state and on nothing else, which is how the app stays as calm as One UI while still changing with every song.

Type: Onest

Onest is an OFL family with open apertures, slightly wide letters and squared-off bowls, which is the feel of One UI's own face without being it. It replaces Figtree, whose round geometric forms are part of what reads as Spotify today. Bold for titles, medium for row titles, regular for everything else.

Shape

Artwork and icon containers are superellipses, drawn with an SVG mask so one class works at any size: .sq up to 96dp, .sq-lg from 100dp, and the rounder .sq-soft for artists. Rectangles come in three radii: 30 for surfaces that float, 26 for groups, 18 for controls nested in a card.

Checked against the things it must not look like

Could it be mistaken forWhat prevents it
SpotifyNo green, no #121212 ramp, no Figtree. No avatar-and-chips header, no short shortcut tiles, no cover-colour gradients, no hairline progress, no five-across transport, no plus-to-check.
Samsung MusicNo Samsung blue or violet, no top text-tab strip of Tracks / Albums / Artists, no attached mini-player strip. Home is trays and cards, which Samsung Music does not have.
A rounded-card templateThree radii with stated jobs and no card shadows. Superellipses in two roundnesses. Colour only on progress and on-state. A dock that is itself a group.

Rules the other sheets follow

  • Viewing area. Root screens (Home, Search, Library, Settings, Recap) open with .hero and collapse to .bar. Detail screens put the cover and title high and the action row under them.
  • Collections are objects, songs are rows. Never put a song on a shelf except in "Recently played", where the brief keeps today's shelf.
  • Groups. Rows live in a .group. The label for a group sits above it on the ground. No gaps between rows, no shadows on cards.
  • Accent. Lamplight marks selection, the one main button on a screen, switches, and progress. Titles stay in ink. Inside .tinted the cover's colour takes over those same jobs and amber does not appear.
  • Shape. Every image and icon container is a superellipse. Nothing is a circle except a radio button, a switch knob and a spinner.

Open questions

  • Amber. Lamplight was chosen because it belongs to the name (an attic lamp, the round window) and to nobody's phone. It sits near the Marigold already in the Recap palette. If the owner wants a cooler app, the accent is two tokens per theme.
  • Native scripts. Onest covers Latin and Cyrillic. Titles tagged in Tamil, Devanagari, Malayalam, Telugu or Kannada would fall back to Noto Sans for that script. If a single family across scripts matters more than the One UI feel, Anek is the candidate.
  • One UI version. The floating capsule tab bar follows One UI 8.5. On One UI 7 the system apps still attach the tab bar to the bottom edge; the dock would look one release ahead there.
