Sheets, dialogs and menus

2026-11-21 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 07

One UI keeps things that need a decision at the bottom of the screen and things that are a short choice beside whatever opened them. This sheet sorts Attic's sheets, dialogs and menus by that rule. The track menu, the playlist picker and Add songs stay bottom sheets, because their length varies or they need a header. The playlist's overflow and the language picker, which is a bottom sheet today, are pop-overs. Naming and deleting a playlist are bottom dialogs with side-by-side text buttons.

It also settles the track menu's layout. Radio, favourite, play next and add to queue are a row of toggles in the shape the Now Playing deck uses, and the rest are rows. Every action keeps its label.

What was kept

  • Every track-menu action and its label: "Start radio", "Add to favourites" / "Remove from favourites" (renamed from liked songs), "Play next", "Add to queue", "Add to playlist", "Language: {name}", "Remove from this playlist" in playlists only, "Go to album" and "Go to artist" only when that album or artist is in the library.
  • How it opens: the kebab or a long press on any song row, a long press on an Instant mixes tile, a top-songs row in Recap or a queue row, and the kebab in Now Playing.
  • Language choices in today's order: "Tamil", "Hindi", "Telugu", "Malayalam", "Kannada", "English", "No vocals". "Unknown" shows in the row when nothing is set, and then no choice is ticked. Tapping a choice saves and closes; tapping outside changes nothing.
  • Playlist picker: "Add to playlist", "New playlist" first, one row per user playlist, opened for one song or for a whole collection, followed by "Added to {name}" or "Already in {name}".
  • Add songs: "Add songs", "Find a song" filtering by title and artist, every library song, tap a row or its button to add or remove at once, the sheet stays open.
  • Name dialog: the field label "Playlist name", "Cancel", and the three title-and-confirm pairs: "Name this playlist" / "Create", "New playlist" / "Create", "Rename playlist" / "Rename". Confirm is disabled while the field is blank.
  • Delete dialog: "Delete {name}?", "The playlist goes away. The songs stay in your library.", "Cancel", "Delete playlist".
  • Overflow: "Rename", "Delete playlist".
  • Every snackbar message, text only, one at a time.

Open questions

  • Actions drawn as toggles. "Start radio", "Play next" and "Add to queue" are one-shot actions, not states; only the heart can be lit. If the row reads as a set of switches, the fallback is the same actions as the first rows of the list, which is today's order.
  • Order. "Remove from this playlist" moves from the middle of the list to the end, where One UI puts the destructive choice. Today it sits before "Go to album".
  • Language as a pop-over on a sheet. It is two floating layers at once. The pop-over is seven rows tall and covers the right half of the track menu while it is open. If that is too much, the picker goes back to being its own sheet, drawn like the playlist picker.
  • Picker row text. "{name} ({n} songs)" is now two lines, "{name}" over "{n} songs". The words are the same; the brackets are gone.
  • New words. The line under "Add songs" ("Late drive, 12 songs") is not in the app today.
  • Sheet colour. A sheet opened over Now Playing, Queue or Lyrics takes their washed surfaces (frame H). Opened anywhere else it is neutral and its lit heart is amber (frame G).
