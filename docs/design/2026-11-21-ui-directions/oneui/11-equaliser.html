11 · Equaliser

2026-11-25 · UI directions · One UI · sheet 11

Attic gets its own ten-band equaliser, and a song or an album can keep a setting of its own. This sheet draws it in the Comfort style: the entry in Now Playing's menu, the sheet in its states, the indicator on Now Playing, and the Settings page that edits the default.

The sheet always edits the setting the song is playing with. Which one that is, is the head of the sheet, and the rows that change it are its foot.

What is new

  • Scope head. The sheet's head names what the bands change: “For all songs”, “For this album”, “For this song only”. The default is a neutral icon box; a song or album of its own shows its cover on the --accent-soft bed the kit uses for the current song.
  • Response well. A card on --fill with the engine's response in the accent, 14 dB either way, dashed lines at ±12.
  • Band sliders. Ten capsule tracks with the switch's white knob and an accent run from 0 dB to the knob. They share the well's ten-octave axis, so each knob is under its bump.
  • Preset chips, the kit's pills in one scrolling row: “Custom” and “Save as preset” first when the bands match no preset, then saved presets, then the eleven built in.
  • Action rows at the foot: “Save for this song”, “Save for this album”, and “Use default” with what it removes on a second line.
  • Indicator. A capsule in Now Playing's header while the song plays with its own or its album's setting.

What was kept

  • The track menu, with “Sleep timer” where it was. “Equaliser” is the next row and puts its state where the timer puts its minutes.
  • The sheet, chips, switch, bottom dialog, pop-over and settings rows are the kit's own. Over the player the sheet takes the cover's wash; in Settings it is Lamplight.
  • “Opens the equaliser on this phone.” keeps its wording and moves to the last card of the new page, under the title “Phone equaliser”.

Open questions

  • Edits made before “Save for this song” stay in the default. The sheet edits what is playing, so someone who moves a knob and then saves for the song has changed every song first. Saving could instead put the default back to what it was when the sheet opened.
  • “Use default” cannot be undone. The toast has no action today. An “Undo” there would make the row safe to tap.
  • Preset curves in these frames are placeholders; the engine owns the real ones.
