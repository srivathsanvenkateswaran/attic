Widget, notifications and launcher icon

2026-11-21 · UI directions · spotify · sheet 10

Attic outside its own window, as it ships today: the home-screen widget in its three layouts, the media notification and lock-screen player, the "Analysing your songs" notification, and the launcher icon. Redrawn from widget/PlayerWidget.kt, the three layout/widget_*.xml files, playback/PlaybackService.kt, analysis/AnalysisWorker.kt and docs/logo.svg, and checked against widget.png, notification.png and lock_screen.png.

Android draws most of this. The wallpaper, quick-settings tiles, clock and notification chrome are plain stand-ins for the phone's own; what Attic supplies is named in each caption. These surfaces use the system font (Roboto here), not Figtree.

Widget measurements

  • Large (250 × 110dp and up). Top area padded 14 and 10: an 84dp cover with 6dp corners, 14dp, then bold 16sp title, 13sp artist at white 80%, and 4dp below them previous (44dp), a 38dp white circle with a black glyph and 12dp side margins, and next (44dp). A 22dp mark box sits top-right; the glyph inside it is 12dp. Below, a black 15% band padded 10 and 8 holds five 44dp-tall tiles with 6dp corners and 4dp side margins.
  • Wide (180 × 50dp and up). A 72dp-wide slice of the cover at full height, 12dp, bold 15sp title over a 13sp artist in #B3B3B3, then three 44dp buttons with white glyphs. The row has no end padding: "next" stops at the widget's edge.
  • Small (110 × 110dp and up). The cover fills the widget, cropped to its shape. A 44dp black 80% circle with a white glyph sits 8dp from the bottom-right corner.
  • Glyphs are the four vectors in res/drawable/widget_*.xml: the sharp Material shapes, not the rounded set the app uses.

The analysis notification carries no Spotify cue. The card, chip, seek line and pause button in B are Android's and look the same for any player.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • A circular launcher mask cuts the house's two lower corners (D). The foreground comment says the mark is "centred in the adaptive safe zone"; it is centred, but its lower corners sit 42dp from the centre and the mask's radius is 36dp.
  • The 22dp mark on the large widget reuses the 108-unit launcher drawing, so the visible glyph is 12dp with 5dp of empty box around it.
  • The widget's album tiles crop each cover to a 44dp-tall strip, which cuts through the artwork's lettering (visible in widget.png too).
  • The small layout at 2 × 2 cells is taller than wide, so the cover loses its left and right edges.
  • The wide layout's artist line is grey #B3B3B3; the large layout's is white at 80%.
  • In the notification the like button is a plus in a circle with no label. Liked, it becomes a filled check in a circle.
  • The analysis notification is not green. No colour is set in AnalysisWorker.kt:118-128.

Guessed, not measured

  • Small and wide widget sizes. No screenshot shows them. They are drawn at 2 × 2 and 4 × 1 of the same cell grid as the large one (163 × 212 and 342 × 100).
  • Analysis notification chrome. No screenshot. The 40dp icon circle and the header line follow the "Android System" row in notification.png; the placement of the title, bar and action follows the standard template and will differ by Android version.
  • Themed icon colours. The launcher takes them from the wallpaper. The two pairs here are stand-ins that match this sheet's wallpaper.
  • Media card colours. Android derives the chip and button colours from the cover. Here they are the fixture cover's pale and deep shades.
  • Widget tiles. The five albums follow the "Recently played" shelf on sheet 01, topped up with the next biggest album; with nothing played, the five albums with the most songs.
  • Seek line. The wave on the played part is one frame of the system's animation.
