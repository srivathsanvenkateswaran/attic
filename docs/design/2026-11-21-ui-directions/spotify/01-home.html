What is on this sheet

One vertically scrolling list. With "All" selected, top to bottom: the header row (36dp settings button, then the four chips), the shortcut grid (two columns of 56dp tiles, Liked Songs always first), the Wrapped card, "Speed dial" (3 × 3 square tiles; a tap starts radio), "Recently played" (shelf of 160dp cards), "Start listening" under its grey overline (four track rows), "Your folders", "Albums", the "More from" spotlight and "Your top artists" (130dp circles).

Where the drawing departs from the screenshots

ScreenshotDrawn hereWhy
home.png, light_home.jpgWrapped card and "Speed dial" between the shortcuts and "Recently played" in frames A, B and DBoth exist in the working tree (ui/HomeScreen.kt:130-153) and postdate the screenshots. Frame E leaves them out and matches home.png.
light_home.jpgMini player artist line is clipped at the edge with no ellipsisThe line is a marquee (ui/NowPlaying.kt:174); the screenshot's "…" predates it.
All390 × 844 frame; the screenshots are 411 × 914dpThe round's frame size. Fewer rows fit; widths and gaps are the code's dp values.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • The settings button and the first chip sit 8dp apart, and the chip row has no left fade: chips scroll out from under the button's right edge.
  • A selected filter other than "All" hides the shortcuts, the Wrapped card, Speed dial, "Recently played", "Start listening" and the spotlight, and leaves most of the screen empty (frame C).
  • The tab bar is a gradient of the background colour, 72% opaque at its top edge, so list content is legible behind the tab icons (frame A, third Speed dial row; home.png, folder names).
  • In light mode the Attic mark in the settings button stays #1ED760 while every other green becomes #12A84A. The mark is a drawable with its own fill.
  • The Wrapped card is plum #2A0E3D in both themes and uses no theme colour.
  • Every row carries the green downloaded badge. It has no state behind it.

Not drawn here

The empty-library state of Home ("No songs in Music/Offline") is on sheet 08 with the other system states.
