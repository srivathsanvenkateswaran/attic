What is on this sheet

The player page is a column with 24dp side padding: header (down chevron, "Playing from" over the context name, kebab), the cover between two equal flexible gaps, the optional lyric line, title and artist with the like button, the seek bar with elapsed and total time, the transport row, and a bottom row with the "Downloaded" label and the queue button. Below it the page scrolls on to three cards with 16dp side padding and 14dp between them.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • In light mode the status-bar icons and the gesture pill are dark on Now Playing (frame D). The activity sets the system-bar style from the app theme (MainActivity.kt:29-33) and Now Playing only swaps the Compose theme.
  • "Downloaded" with its badge is always shown and never changes. It is the one green element on a playing screen with shuffle and repeat off.
  • The first card starts directly under the player page with no gap of its own; the space above it is the page's navigation-bar padding.
  • The time on the right is the total length. There is no time-remaining display.
  • The mini player's stated height in the layout maths is 64dp, and the bar plus its 4dp gap measures 68dp, so lists end 4dp closer to it than the padding intends.

Where the drawing departs from the screenshots

now_playing.png was taken on a 411 × 914dp phone, so its cover is larger and the gaps above and below it are taller. Every fixed measure is the same. The screenshot shows shuffle on; frame A shows both toggles off and frame C shows them on. lyrics_card.png matches frame B from the title down.
