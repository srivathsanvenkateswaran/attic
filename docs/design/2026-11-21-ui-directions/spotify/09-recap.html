Wrapped

2026-11-21 · UI directions · spotify · sheet 09

Wrapped as it ships today: the page, the story and its ten cards, and the card on Home that opens it. Redrawn from ui/WrappedScreen.kt, ui/WrappedCards.kt and ui/HomeScreen.kt. The file is called 09-recap so the four folders share file names; in this folder the feature keeps its current name.

No screenshot of Wrapped exists. Every frame here is built from the code's measurements, colours and strings, filled with the round's fixture numbers. The cards have a fixed palette of their own and follow neither the app theme nor the cover art.

What is on this sheet

The page is one scrolling list under a back bar and four period chips: the banner, six stat tiles, "Share a card" (118dp thumbnails of the same cards), "Top songs" with a play circle, "Top artists", "Languages", "When you listen" and a footnote. The tab bar and mini player stay. The story is a full-screen black dialog over everything: one progress segment per card, the card at 9:16 with 12dp side margins and 10dp corners, and three pill buttons. A card stays 7 seconds; a tap on its left third goes back, a tap elsewhere goes on, a hold pauses.

Frames C1 to C10 are the story cut down to the segments and the card, at the size the story draws it on this phone: 366 × 651dp, the 360 × 640 canvas scaled by 1.017. Frame D is the uncut screen.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • Card 5 reads "3,120 minutes over 742 plays". Minutes are grouped with a comma and play counts are not (ui/WrappedCards.kt:137), so a count over 999 would print as "1048 plays".
  • On card 5 "Your top artist" is printed over the lower edge of the artist image. The text block is anchored 84dp above the card's bottom and grows upwards.
  • On card 2 the mint circle reaches behind the last digits of the count.
  • Card 9 shows "412 songs played for the first time", the same number as the year's song count. The log began on 3 January 2026, so every song's first play falls in this year.
  • "No vocals" is drawn in plum #2A0E3D on the page's language bar and legend dot, against #121212 in dark mode.
  • The artist image in "Top artists" and on cards 5 and 6 is the cover of that artist's most played song this period, skipping covers a higher-ranked artist already has (ui/WrappedScreen.kt:85-93). A. R. Rahman therefore shows Rockstar here; the round's fixture gives him Anbe Aaruyire on the other sheets.
  • Card 10's footer and headline say the same thing twice on the year: "Your 2026 so far" above, "2026 so far" below.
  • From 24 December the headline on card 10 becomes "Your 2026 Wrapped". Its column is 120dp wide and "Wrapped" at 34sp is about 133dp, so Android breaks the word in two (frame E, last strip). This is worked out from the font's widths and has not been seen on a device.
  • The same headline on the page banner wraps to two lines from 24 December (frame E, third strip).
  • The banner, the Home card and every story card ignore the light theme (frame F).
  • "Save" reports through the app's snackbar ("Saved to Pictures/Attic"). The snackbar belongs to the main window and the story is a dialog window above it, so by the code the message is covered until the story closes. It is not drawn in frame D.

Guessed, not measured

  • Vertical positions inside the cards. Computed from Figtree's metrics (ascent 0.95em, descent 0.25em) on the reading that card text uses Compose's default line-height trimming, since the styles in ui/Theme.kt set none. Under that reading "2026" on card 1 stops 10dp above the bottom edge and does not run off it, though the code comment says it should. A device screenshot would settle it.
  • Line breaks are the browser's. Android may break "Your year so far", "A. R. Rahman" or the summary headline differently.
  • Numbers the fixture does not give. Ranks 6 to 10 of both lists; A. R. Rahman's 742 plays; card 9's "312 min on Saturday 15 August" and "11×"; the hour-by-hour spread behind the clock, which was made to peak at 10 pm with its busiest three hours from 9 pm.
  • Segment progress. The current segment is drawn 40% full in C1 to C10.
  • Late-December numbers. Frame E keeps the 21 November figures so that only the wording differs.

Not drawn here

The loading spinner between periods, the other three periods' wording ("Your week in music", "Your November in music", "Everything you've played") and the system share sheet. Their strings are in CURRENT-UI.md section 2.16.
