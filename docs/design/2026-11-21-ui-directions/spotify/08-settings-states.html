What is on this sheet

Settings is one scrolling column under a back arrow and a title: three radio rows for the theme, a "Lyrics" section with a bulk download, a "Song radio" section with a switch and the analysis progress, and an "About" paragraph. It is an ordinary destination, so the mini player and tab bar stay. The other frames are the launch states (permission, loading), Home with an empty library, and a collection that no longer exists.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • The progress track is lilac (#4A4458 dark, #E8DEF8 light). The theme sets no secondaryContainer, so Material's default shows. It is the only colour on any screen that belongs to neither the grey ramp nor the green.
  • The two status lines under progress bars are in the primary text colour while every paragraph around them is grey, so the smallest text in the section is the brightest.
  • The analysis progress bar is always there, full or not. The lyrics progress bar exists only during a download.
  • "Download lyrics for all songs" vanishes while a download runs and there is no cancel.
  • On opening, the bottom third of the page is under the mini player and tab bar (frame G).
  • Home with an empty library draws no header at all (frame D2). Search and Your Library keep theirs and are the way to Settings from there.
  • The empty message centres each line as a box. A line that wraps takes the full width and reads left-aligned under a centred title (frame D2). The permission screen sets centred text and does not have this.
  • The missing-item screen puts the message at the top with 48dp above it; Home centres the same block in the screen. Its back arrow has no title beside it and sits at the very edge.
  • The mark on the permission screen is #1ED760 in light mode too, beside a button in #12A84A.

Spotify cues on this sheet

  • "Song radio" and "Autoplay", with Autoplay's description (ui/SettingsScreen.kt:118-132).
  • The green: every Material control here takes primary, so the radio button, switch, progress bar, text buttons and the filled "Allow access" button are all #1ED760 on #121212 with #B3B3B3 secondary text.
  • Figtree with bold, tight section titles.
  • The mark on the permission screen is the launcher glyph, green on near-black.
  • Under the page: the tinted floating mini player with the plus-in-circle, and the fading tab bar with "Your Library".

The structure of Settings, the permission screen, the spinner and the empty messages are not Spotify's. What makes them read that way is colour and type.

Guessed, not measured

  • The stock controls as Material 3 draws them for the library version in use: the radio button, the switch with its outlined off state, the progress bar's 4dp gap and stop dot, and the spinner's arc. The radio buttons match light_mode_emulator.png; no screenshot shows the others.
  • The 48dp row a text button occupies. Checked against the screenshot's spacing between the small print, the button and "About".
  • The counts: 38 songs with lyrics, 84 then 91 analysed, 52 checked and 23 found mid-download.

Where the drawing departs from the screenshot

light_mode_emulator.png shows Appearance, Lyrics and About on a 411dp-wide phone with 67 songs. Frame B adds "Song radio" between Lyrics and About (ui/SettingsScreen.kt:118-160), uses the fixture's 127 songs, and is 390dp wide, so the paragraphs break at different words.

Not drawn here
