Album, artist and playlist

2026-11-21 · UI directions · spotify · sheet 04

Album, Artist, Folder, Playlist and Liked Songs as they ship today, redrawn from ui/CollectionScreens.kt and ui/Components.kt. This is the baseline the three proposals are compared against, not a design. Where the current screen is awkward it is drawn awkward.

All five share one frame: a list under a top bar that starts transparent and takes the cover's tint on scroll, a header, and one control row. Covers are the generated fixtures, so each tint is the app's tint rule applied to the fixture cover. album.png and artist.png are the screenshots to compare frames A and B with.

What is on this sheet

Every screen is one list drawn edge to edge under the status bar, with the top bar laid over it. The first list item is the header plus the control row. Rows follow, then the app's bottom padding so the last row clears the mini player and tab bar.

Spotify cues on these screens

  • The header gradient from the cover's colour into the background, behind a centred cover (ui/CollectionScreens.kt:136-141).
  • The top bar that takes the cover colour and reveals the title on scroll (:76-103).
  • The control row: small cover thumbnail, plus-in-circle and green download arrow at the left; shuffle and a large green play circle at the right (:189-231).
  • The artist hero: a full-bleed image with the name in very large bold white type over a dark fade (:310-327).
  • "Popular" with numbered rows, and "Popular releases" (:331-352).
  • The small round artist image beside the album's artist name (:165-177).
  • "Album • 2026" and "6 songs • 26 min" with a bullet separator (:269, :285).
  • "Liked Songs" and its violet-to-mint tile with a white heart.
  • "Let's find something for your playlist" (:529).
  • In the rows: the green badge before every artist, the current title in green with equaliser bars, the trailing kebab, 4dp cover corners.
  • The tab bar and the mini player, as on every sheet.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • The control row's thumbnail repeats the cover that is directly above it, cropped to 34 × 44, and is not tappable. The badge next to it is the same decoration as in the rows.
  • The plus-in-circle in the control row adds the whole collection to a playlist. The same glyph in the mini player likes one song.
  • Playlist screens have two plus buttons side by side: plus-in-circle (add this playlist's songs to another playlist) and a bare plus (add songs to this one).
  • "Popular" has no play counts behind it. It is the artist's songs by how recently they were played, then by title, which is why four of the five in frame B start with A to J.
  • The artist image is whichever song of theirs sorts first, so it is an album cover with its lettering, cropped.
  • The status bar sits directly on the hero image with no scrim (frame B, and artist.png).
  • In light mode the back arrow is #121212 on a black 35% disc (frame G).
  • An empty collection keeps a full-size green play circle that does nothing (frames J and K), and reports "0 songs, 0 min".
  • Liked Songs takes its header colour from the first liked song, so the screen's tint changes whenever a song is liked.
  • The empty messages are centred as blocks. A line that wraps fills the width and reads left-aligned, because the code sets no text alignment (frame J).
  • The bar collapses on a fixed 520-pixel scroll, about 198dp on the owner's phone, while the cover's bottom edge is still some 140dp further down the header. Between those two points the tinted bar sits over the lower part of the cover (frame F shows the last 5dp of it).
  • On a tall phone a short album cannot scroll 520 pixels, so its bar never collapses. Frame F does at 390 × 844 because six rows and the footer leave 241dp of scroll.
  • Rows scroll under the mini player and show through the tab bar.

Guessed

  • The collapse point is in pixels, so its dp value depends on the screen density. Frames D, F and I are past it at any phone density.
  • Durations the fixture does not give: "12 songs, 47 min" (Late drive), "31 songs, 2 hr 8 min" (Liked Songs), "46 songs, 3 hr 24 min" (Hindi).
  • Which songs are in Late drive, in Liked Songs and in the Hindi folder, beyond the albums the fixture's mosaics imply.
  • The fixture names 13 of A. R. Rahman's 14 songs. Frame I is cut by the mini player before the list ends.
  • The dragged row in frame D is drawn in its slot with the grey fill. On the phone it also follows the finger.
  • Frame J reuses the name "Late drive" for the just-created state. The fixture has no empty playlist.

Not drawn here

The missing-item state ("This album is gone") is on sheet 08. The playlist kebab's dropdown, the rename and delete dialogs, the Add songs sheet, the playlist picker and the track menu with "Remove from this playlist" are on sheet 07.
