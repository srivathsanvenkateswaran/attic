What is on this sheet

Both screens are full-screen overlays that slide up over Now Playing and close with a down chevron at the top left. The Queue is a list on the app's background: a 48dp header ("Queue" in titleLarge, "Clear" as a text button), two small grey headings, the current song, then the songs to come. Full-screen lyrics is the cover tint edge to edge with a centred title and artist, the lines, and a play circle.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • Tapping the current song in the Queue does nothing, though it looks like every other row.
  • The Queue has no sign of radio (frame B) and no history of played songs.
  • The swipe strip says "Remove" at its right end whichever way the row is swiped, so on a swipe to the right the word is uncovered last.
  • In light mode the Queue is white (frame G) between two screens that stay dark.
  • Unsung lyric lines are black at 75% over a tint whose lightness is capped at 30%. On the fixture's brown they are harder to read than on the blue of lyrics_full.png.
  • Full-screen lyrics has no seek bar, no skip and no way to the queue. The header's right side is an empty 48dp slot.
  • With nothing saved the lyrics screen shows the button and no explanation (frame I); the card shows a sentence above the same button.
  • While downloading, the card keeps whichever sentence it had and puts a spinner where the button was.
  • The instrumental card has no button and no pill, and is the shortest of the six.

Spotify cues on this sheet

  • Full-screen lyrics: the screen filled with the cover colour, large bold lines, white for sung and dark for upcoming (ui/LyricsScreen.kt:59-60, 176-254).
  • The lyrics card: a coloured card under the player with a white "Show lyrics" pill (ui/LyricsScreen.kt:90-155), and the string "Show lyrics".
  • In the Queue rows: the current song's title in green with equaliser bars, the green down-arrow badge before every artist, the trailing kebab, 4dp cover corners (ui/Components.kt:90-196).
  • The down chevron as the close control for a full-screen player layer, and the white play circle with a black glyph.
  • The accent green on "Clear" and "Download lyrics", on the #121212 and #B3B3B3 neutrals, in Figtree.

Guessed, not measured

  • Frame D's scroll position. The code scrolls so that the line two before the current one is the first item; the frame puts that item at the top of the 16dp content padding.
  • How far the swiped row in frame F has travelled, and that the dragged row is drawn in its original slot.
  • The spinner is one frame of the stock indeterminate indicator.
  • The 4dp above and below "Download lyrics" in the card. Material lays a 40dp button out in a 48dp slot; the same rule matches the text buttons in the Settings screenshot.
  • The up-next songs in frame B. Radio picks them by audio similarity and language; these are ten Hindi songs from the fixture library.

Where the drawing departs from the screenshots

lyrics_full.png is 411 × 914dp and shows a song whose lines mostly fit one line of Devanagari. The fixture lines are English and most wrap to two, so fewer are in view. Sizes, padding and colours are the same. lyrics_card.png matches the synced card on sheet 05 and the last strip of frame E.

Not drawn here

The inline lyric line above the title is on sheet 05, frame B. The track menu that a kebab or a long-press on a queue row opens is on sheet 07. The snackbars a lyrics download can raise ("No lyrics found for {title}", "Connect to the internet to download lyrics") are on sheet 07 with the other messages.
