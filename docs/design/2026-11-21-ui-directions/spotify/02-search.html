What is on this sheet

A column under the status bar: the header row (36dp settings button, 12dp, "Search" in 26/31 bold; padding start 16, top 10, bottom 12), the search field (56dp tall, 16dp side margins, radius 6, no underline), then one of three bodies. Empty query: "Start browsing" in 16/21 medium, then a two-column grid of 92dp tiles with 10dp gaps. A query with matches: one list, 8dp top padding, artists (up to 5), folders, songs, albums (up to 10). A query with none: the empty message.

Spotify cues on this screen

  • A white search field on a dark screen, with the placeholder "What do you want to listen to?" (ui/SearchScreen.kt:121, 182-197).
  • "Start browsing" over coloured category tiles, each with a cover tucked into the bottom-right corner at 25° and clipped by the tile (:147, 199-228).
  • The tile colours themselves: #8D67AB, #E13300, #1E3264, #148A08, #BA5D07, #503750, #0D73EC, #AF2896 (:91-94).
  • Result rows with a type word as the subtitle: "Artist", "Playlist", "Album, {artist}"; round covers for artists and square for everything else.
  • In the song rows: the green down-arrow badge before every artist, the current song's title in green with equaliser bars, and a trailing kebab.
  • The round avatar-style button beside the screen title, the "Home / Search / Your Library" tab bar with no solid fill, and the floating tinted mini player with the plus-in-circle.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • Result groups have no headings and a fixed order, with albums last. A query that matches an album by name also matches every song on it through the album name, so the album row lands under all of its own songs (frames B and F).
  • Folders are labelled "Playlist". User playlists ("Late drive", "Rahman, slow") are not searched at all.
  • A folder's result row and browse tile show the cover of its first song, where Home and Your Library show a four-cover mosaic for the same folder.
  • Artist and album result rows have no kebab and no badge; song rows have both. The two row types also differ in cover size, 56dp against 50dp, so text columns do not line up between them (frame E).
  • The browse tiles' titles are white on fixed colours in both themes. The tile colour is picked by position, so the same album changes colour when a folder is added.
  • Rows scroll under the mini player and show through the tab bar.

Where the drawing departs from the brief

The brief asks for frame B to mix an artist, albums, a playlist and songs for "dhur". With the fixture library the app returns no artist and no folder for that query: artists and folders are matched on their own names only (ui/SearchScreen.kt:85-87). Frame B shows what the app returns. Frame E uses "ph", which does return all four types.

Guessed

  • Frames B, D, E and F show a typed query with the cursor and no keyboard. That is the state after the keyboard is dismissed with back: the field keeps focus. While the keyboard is up the list is shortened to end above it (imePadding, :112). The keyboard is the system's and is not drawn.
  • Which albums make the browse grid depends on song counts. The fixture gives counts for the songs it names only, so the eight shown are the biggest by those, ties in name order, as the code would break them.
  • Songs the fixture lists without an artist are given their album's artist.
  • The cover of an artist the brief does not list (phonk.me) is their first song's cover, which is the rule the app uses.

Not drawn here

The track menu opened by a row's kebab is on sheet 07. Search with an empty library has no layout of its own: the browse grid is simply empty under "Start browsing".
