Sheets, dialogs and messages

2026-11-21 · UI directions · spotify · sheet 07

The bottom sheets, dialogs, dropdown and snackbar as they ship today, redrawn from ui/Sheets.kt, ui/CollectionScreens.kt and ui/AppRoot.kt. None of them has a screenshot. The app's own parts (rows, header, labels) use the code's measurements; the sheet, dialog, dropdown and snackbar containers are stock Material 3 and are drawn with its defaults.

Frames A to C are one path: the menu for "Nadaan Parindey", then the two pickers it leads to. The playlist behind frames D to F1 is "Late drive" as sheet 04 draws it.

What is on this sheet

The modal sheets share one container: a drag handle, rounded top corners, surfaceContainer fill and a scrim over the screen behind. The track menu has a song header and a divider; the two pickers have a title; Add songs has a title, a filter field and a list. Two alert dialogs and a dropdown manage playlists. Messages arrive in a snackbar.

Things that look odd and are correct

  • Like is a heart in the track menu and a plus or check on every other surface.
  • The language row is the only one that carries a value in its label ("Language: Hindi").
  • The language picker's order is Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, English, No vocals. The check that marks the current choice is the same grey as the globes.
  • In the playlist picker a playlist that already has the song is marked with a check and still accepts the tap; the result is the message "Already in {name}".
  • The rename dialog opens with the caret before the existing name, not after it and not with the name selected.
  • "Delete playlist" is not styled as destructive.
  • Add songs has two fills of the same family: the sheet is surfaceContainer and the field is surfaceContainerHighest. Its field looks nothing like the Search tab's.
  • The snackbar covers content. Over Now Playing it sits on the shuffle and skip buttons (frame K).
  • In light mode the sheets, dialogs and dropdown are pale over a player that stays dark (frame H).
  • Liking, removing from a playlist, renaming and deleting show no message. Add songs shows none either, though adding from the picker does.

Spotify cues on this sheet

  • The track menu as a whole: a dark bottom sheet with a cover-title-artist header, a divider, and icon-plus-label rows ending in "Go to album" and "Go to artist" (ui/Sheets.kt:98-156).
  • Its strings: "Start radio", "Add to liked songs" / "Remove from liked songs", "Add to queue", "Add to playlist", "Go to album", "Go to artist" (ui/Sheets.kt:116-150).
  • The #282828 sheet on #121212 with #B3B3B3 icons, green as the one accent, Figtree.
  • In the rows of Add songs and of the screens behind: the green down-arrow badge, the trailing kebab, 4dp cover corners.
  • Behind the sheets: the floating tinted mini player, the fading tab bar, the collection header and control row. Those are covered on sheets 01, 04 and 05.

The pickers, the dialogs, the dropdown and the snackbar are stock Material and carry no cue of their own beyond colour and type. The create dialog's title "Give your playlist a name" is a cue; it is on sheet 03.

Guessed, not measured

  • Everything Material draws by default: the 32 × 4dp handle and its 22dp margins, 28dp top corners, the 32% sheet scrim, the 60% dim and 28dp corners of dialogs, the 4dp corners and shadows of the dropdown and the snackbar, and the dialog's 328dp width on this frame. Text buttons in the dialogs are drawn 40dp tall as the kit has them; Material lays each out in a 48dp slot, which would make both dialogs 8dp taller.
  • The height of Add songs. The code gives its content 92% of the height the sheet offers; the kit draws the whole sheet at 92% of the screen. The real sheet may be some 30dp shorter.
  • The dropdown's position: under the kebab with its right edge on the kebab's right edge, where Material's position rule puts a menu that does not fit to the right of its anchor.
  • The caret position in the rename dialog. The field is given a plain string, and Compose starts such a field with the caret at index 0. Not checked on a phone.
  • The keyboard in frames E1 and J: its height (264dp including the gesture area), its keys and its colours are a stand-in for whatever keyboard the phone uses. The dialog's vertical position follows from that height.

Not drawn here

The create form of the name dialog ("Give your playlist a name", from the Library's plus) is on sheet 03, frame D. The picker opened for a whole album, artist or folder from a control row is the same sheet as frame B. With no playlists the picker is its title and the "New playlist" row. A song whose language the app cannot tell reads "Language: Unknown" and its picker has no check; every song in the fixture library sits in a folder that names its language, so that state does not occur here. The other snackbar messages are listed in CURRENT-UI.md section 2.17 and look the same.
