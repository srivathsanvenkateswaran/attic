07 · Sheets, dialogs, menu and snackbar

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 07

The sheets, dialogs, menu and snackbar are Material's own components, so the work here is in what goes inside them. The track menu is the one that changes most: today it is a header, a divider and nine identical rows, and here it is sorted into playing the song, filing it, and going somewhere else.

Every action from section 2.13 is present with its label, through the naming table: “Add to favourites” and “Remove from favourites”, “Name this playlist”.

What was kept

  • Every track-menu action and its result: “Start radio”, the favourite toggle, “Play next”, “Add to queue”, “Add to playlist”, “Language: {name}”, “Remove from this playlist” inside a user playlist, “Go to album” and “Go to artist” when they are in the library.
  • “Add to playlist” and “Language” replace the sheet with their picker. Tapping a playlist or a language acts and closes. Dismissing changes nothing.
  • The seven languages in the code's order, and “Unknown” as the unset state.
  • “New playlist” first in the picker, opening the name dialog titled “New playlist”. A playlist that already holds the song can still be tapped and answers “Already in {name}”.
  • Add songs: 92% of the screen height, “Find a song”, the whole library by title, tap the row or the button to toggle, the sheet stays open.
  • The name dialog's three forms, the “Playlist name” label, focus on open, the disabled confirm. The delete dialog's title, sentence and buttons.
  • The menu's two items, and every snackbar string.

Open questions

  • Which three actions lead the track menu. The button group holds the three that play the song. The favourite toggle is the other candidate: it could take the place of “Start radio”, or sit in the header as the heart from the mini player with no label.
  • The names under “Go to album” and “Go to artist”, the line under each sheet title, and the note in the language picker are new text. Each can be removed without moving anything else.
  • A sheet opened from the player takes the cover's scheme (frame H). If sheets should look the same wherever they open, frame A's colours apply there too.
  • The snackbar over the player (frame J) lands on the tools bar, as it lands on the transport today. Raising it clear of the bar, or showing these messages at the top of the player, would fix that and is a behaviour change.
  • Add songs has no “Done”. It closes by the handle, the scrim or back, as today. A full-height sheet that stays open while you tap usually has an explicit way out.
  • The check in the playlist picker says “already in” without a word. A line such as “9 songs, has this song” would say it.
