02 · Search

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 02

Search is built on the Material search bar. At rest it sits under the title with Browse below it. With focus it moves to the top of the screen, takes a back arrow, and the page becomes results: the search view Pixel's own apps use, in place of today's white field over a grid of coloured tiles.

Results are grouped by what a tap does. Things you open (artists, albums, folders) share the first group, and the shape of each says what it is. Songs you play come next in their own group. The app has no result-type filter and this sheet adds none.

What was kept

  • Live results on every keystroke, ignoring case and accents. No submit step, no history, no suggestions and no result-type filter.
  • What is searched (songs, artists, albums, folders) and how matches rank within each kind. The limits of five artists and ten albums.
  • The clear button, which appears once there is text.
  • Tapping a song plays every matching song from that row with the context “Search results”. The kebab and a long-press open the track menu.
  • Browse: every folder first, then the eight albums with the most songs. A tap opens the folder or album.
  • The no-results strings, and the labels “Artist” and “Album, {artist}”.
  • The screen resizes above the keyboard; the mini player and navigation bar are present throughout.

About the fixtures in frames B and D

No artist or folder in the fixture library contains “dhur”. The real result is two albums and ten songs, which is what the baseline sheet draws. The brief asks frame B to show an artist, albums, a playlist and songs, so B and D add two items that are not in the fixture: an artist, “Madhur Sharma”, and a folder, “Dhurandhar gym”. Frame E uses “ph”, which returns all four kinds with nothing added.

Open questions

  • Five of the eight albums are a swipe away. The carousel shows three covers; today's grid shows every name once you scroll. If seeing all eight at once matters more, the kit's 72dp browse tile lays them out as a two-column grid under the folders, at the cost of a page that looks more like today's.
  • Result order. Albums move from last to second so that an album sits above its own tracks. Songs still fill most of the screen for a query like “dhur”. The alternative keeps today's order inside the new groups.
  • A title above the search bar. “Search” costs 72dp and matches the other two roots. The bar could instead be the app bar, with the gear inside its trailing edge where Google's apps put the account picture.
  • Folders read “Folder” in results. The feature contract lists “Playlist” as the result label. This follows the Library rows in this folder, where a folder and a playlist you made are told apart.
  • User playlists are not searched today, and are not here. If they should be, they join the first group with the label “Playlist, {n} songs”.
  • The mini player above the keyboard leaves room for about four results while typing. It could hide while the keyboard is up and return when the list is scrolled.
