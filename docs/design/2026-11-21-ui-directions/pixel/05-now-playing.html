05 · Now Playing and the mini player

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 05

The player takes a full colour scheme from the cover, the way the Pixel media player does, instead of fading the cover's colour into black. Every component on it is the same one the rest of the app uses, drawn with roles that .from-art rebuilds from the cover's tint.

This sheet settles how playing and paused look, and where the controls go now that the plus-in-a-circle, the white play circle and the green dots are gone.

What was kept

  • “Playing from” and every context name, including “Radio · {song title}”.
  • Close button and swipe down to dismiss; the track menu; swipe the cover sideways to change song; tap the artist to open the artist.
  • Tap and drag to seek, elapsed and total time (not time remaining).
  • Shuffle on and off; repeat off, all, one; previous, play or pause, next; open queue.
  • The inline lyric line, shown only when the song has synced lyrics; the lyrics card; “About the artist”; “Credits” with “Performed by”, “Album”, “Year”; the compact header once scrolled.
  • The first page is one screen tall, with the top of the lyrics card showing above the gesture bar as the cue to scroll.

Motion on this screen

  • Play and pause. The button morphs between pill (paused, 152 wide, fully round) and rounded square (playing, 128 wide, 28 corners) on the fast spatial spring; the previous and next buttons give way and settle. The glyph cross-fades on the fast effects spring.
  • The wave. While playing the wave travels toward the handle at one wavelength a second. On pause its amplitude springs to zero over about 300 ms; on play it grows back. While dragging, it flattens so the position is easy to read.
  • The cover. Scale and corner radius follow the same spring as the button. Changing song slides the pager one item; the sliver grows into the large slot.
  • Open and close. The mini player's cover is a shared element: it grows into the pager while the pill's container expands into the full surface. Predictive back plays the same transition in reverse under the finger.

Open questions

  • Favourite has moved from beside the title to the tools row, next to queue. It is closer to the thumb and it frees the title, but it is no longer next to the song name. The alternative keeps it at the end of the title line.
  • Light Now Playing. Frame D follows the theme. If the player should stay dark at all times, as today, the dark art scheme can be forced on this screen alone.
  • The sliver of the next song. When the queue is one album, the sliver is a strip of the same cover (frame A). It still marks the swipe; frame C shows it with a different cover.
