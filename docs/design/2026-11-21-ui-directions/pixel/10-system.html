10 · Widget, notifications and launcher icon

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 10

Attic outside its own window. The widget is the one surface here that Attic draws in full, and it is built from the mini player's parts: a container in the cover's scheme, the play control that squares off while playing, the progress ring. The media notification and the lock-screen player are drawn by Android from what the app hands it; the frames show them as Android 16 draws them on a Pixel, and the captions say which parts are Attic's. The launcher icon is a container for the mark, which is being redrawn separately.

Wallpapers, quick-settings tiles, the clock and neighbouring app icons are stand-ins for the phone's own.

Widget measures

  • Large, 4 × 2 (358 × 201dp here). 16dp padding. Cover 96dp with 20dp corners. Title 18/24 bold, artist 14/20, one line each. Under them: previous, play or pause, next. The mark at 20dp in the top-right corner. 14dp below, five covers of equal width (59dp here) with 12dp corners, 8dp apart.
  • Wide, 4 × 1 (358 × 88dp). Cover 64dp with 16dp corners, 12dp from the edge. Title 16/22 bold, artist 13/18. The three controls at the right, previous and next in the 40dp narrow width.
  • Small, 2 × 2 (171 × 196dp). 10dp padding. Cover 151 × 118dp with 18dp corners. Under it the title, 13/16 bold on up to two lines, and the play control.
  • The play control is the mini player's: a 36dp button in a 52dp ring, 18dp radius paused and 11dp playing. Previous and next are 40dp tonal circles in 48dp targets.
  • Type is the system font, as today. On a Pixel that is Google Sans, so the widget matches the app without bundling a font into it.

What was kept

  • One widget that changes layout as it is resized; 4 × 2 by default, 110 × 50dp at the smallest; the picker text “Your song and controls, with your recent albums. Resize it for a smaller player.”
  • The controls at each size: cover and play or pause (small); cover, title, artist, previous, play or pause, next (wide); the same plus five album shortcuts and the mark (large). Descriptions “Play or pause”, “Next”, “Previous”, “Open album”.
  • The five tiles are the most recently played albums, topped up with the albums that have the most songs. A tile opens its album; a tap anywhere else opens the app.
  • With no song the widget reads “Attic” and “Tap to open”. After a reboot it shows the last song, paused.
  • The media notification's contents: title, artist, album, the cover read from the file, the small icon, previous, play or pause, next, the seek position, and the two custom actions. It appears once playback starts and opens the app on tap.
  • The analysis notification: silent and ongoing on “Song analysis”, “Analysing your songs”, “{done} of {total}”, a determinate bar, “Stop”, and the app on tap.
  • An adaptive launcher icon with a monochrome layer.

Open questions

  • The ring on the widget. Today the widget is redrawn when the song or the play state changes. A ring that moves needs a redraw every few seconds while music plays. If that costs too much battery, the widget keeps the control's change of shape and loses the ring.
  • The icon's colours. A colour launcher icon cannot read the wallpaper, so these are fixed: the light primaryContainer, primary and onPrimary of the fallback seed #3F6FD8 from sheet 00. If the fallback seed changes, or the new mark arrives with a colour of its own, the icon follows it.
  • Plate or no plate. The eight-lobed plate gives the icon a shape that does not depend on the glyph. Google's own Pixel icons are a glyph on a flat circle; that is the plainer option, and the themed icon then becomes the bare glyph.
  • The cut-out in the themed icon has to stay legible at 48dp. That depends on the new mark; a mark with thin strokes would need the plain glyph as its monochrome layer.
  • The widget follows the theme. Today it is always the cover's tint with white text. Here a light phone gets the cover's light roles (frame E).
  • A title in the small size. Today the small widget has no text. The inset cover leaves a row beside the control and the title fills it.
  • Android 16's progress style. The analysis notification could use Notification.ProgressStyle, which on a Pixel also puts a chip in the status bar. That is more presence than a background job needs, so the standard template is kept.
