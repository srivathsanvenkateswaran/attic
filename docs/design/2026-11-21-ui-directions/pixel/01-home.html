01 · Home

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 01

Home is rebuilt from Material's containers instead of re-skinned. Today's page is a profile-style button, a row of pill chips, a two-column grid of short tiles and then identical shelves, which is the layout of one streaming app. This version keeps every section and every string, and gives each kind of content a different component.

Colour on this screen comes from the wallpaper scheme (seed #3F6FD8 in these mocks). Only the mini player takes its colour from the cover.

What was kept

  • Every section and its order: shortcuts, Recap card, Instant mixes, Recently played, Start listening, Your folders, Albums, More from, Top artists.
  • The four filters, one always selected, and what each leaves on the page.
  • Favourites first among the shortcuts; up to six shortcuts; up to nine instant mixes; four random songs.
  • Tap targets and results: a shortcut opens its collection, a mix pill starts radio (long-press opens the track menu), a recent song plays the recents list.
  • The mini player's controls: favourite, play or pause, swipe the title for next and previous, tap to open Now Playing.

Open questions

  • Six shortcuts, two and a half visible. The carousel shows Favourites, one large cover and part of the next; the rest are a swipe away. Today's grid shows all six at once. If seeing six matters more than the large cover, the fallback is two rows of three covers in the same component.
  • Favourites as a pill with a count and no word. The heart carries the meaning and the label is read out by TalkBack. If that is too terse, the pill widens to hold “Favourites”.
  • “Albums” as a grid. Frame C lays the filtered albums out as a grid. The contract says the filter leaves “only the Albums shelf”; the content is the same 14 albums.
