What was kept

  • One frame for album, artist, folder, playlist and Favourites, with a header that collapses to the title on scroll.
  • Play or pause for the collection (plays from the first song with shuffle off when the collection is not the current one), shuffle with an on state, and adding the whole collection to a playlist. All three are disabled on an empty collection.
  • Album: the artist link, “Album” or “Single” with the year, tracks in track order without covers, the count and duration.
  • Artist: five ranked songs, the artist's albums, the remaining songs under “All songs”, one queue for the whole artist.
  • Folder: read-only, with “Playlist from your Music/Offline folder”.
  • Playlist: add songs, reorder by dragging a handle, rename and delete from the overflow, and “Remove from this playlist” in the track menu (sheet 07).
  • Favourites: newest first, and its empty state. A playlist's empty state.
  • Rows: tap plays in context, long-press or the kebab opens the track menu. No durations and no sort control, as today.

Where this sheet departs from the kit

The kit's .chead, shown on the direction sheet, puts the cover first and the actions in a row under the text. With the title on one line that header is 540dp tall: two tracks show above the mini player, and the third, the one that is playing, is behind it. This sheet keeps the kit's surface, type and buttons and changes the order: title, byline, then the cover with the cluster beside it. The header is 464dp and three tracks show. The rules are in this sheet's <style> (.chead.tf, .cstage, .ccluster, .cplay). If the arrangement is accepted they belong in kit.css, and the two header strips on sheet 00 should follow.

Open questions

  • The size of Play. At 134 by 144dp it is the largest control in the app, larger than the 128 by 80 button in Now Playing. It could drop to 80dp tall with the song count and duration set above it in the cluster.
  • A play control in the collapsed bar. Today the collapsed bar has only the back arrow and the title. Frame F adds play or pause for the collection, which is otherwise out of reach until you scroll back up. Shuffle and add-to-playlist stay in the header.
  • “Top songs” has no counts. Today's “Popular” is the five most recently played, not the five most played. The new name promises play counts, and the listening log that Recap uses could supply them. That is a behaviour change, so this sheet shows ranks only.
  • Two empty-state strings mention a plus. “Tap the plus on a song to save it” is wrong once the like control is a heart; frame J says “Tap the heart”. “Tap the plus above to add songs from your library” still holds because “Add songs” carries a plus, but “Tap Add songs above” would be plainer. Neither string is in the brief's naming table.
  • Reorder hides the header. It gives the list the whole screen and makes the way out obvious. The alternative keeps the header and turns “Reorder” into a filled “Done” in place.
  • The labelled tools row costs one song. A playlist shows two rows under the header where an album shows three. Moving “Add songs” and “Reorder” into the bar as icons would win the row back and bring back the unlabelled plus.
  • Count and duration appear twice on an album, in the header and under the last track. The footer is kept because the contract lists it; it could go.
