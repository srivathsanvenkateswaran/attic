00 · Direction: Material 3 Expressive on Pixel

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 00

The proposal is Material 3 Expressive as Google ships it on Pixel in Android 16: dynamic colour, Google Sans Flex, the expressive shape set, button groups, the short navigation bar, grouped lists and the wavy progress track. The aim is an app a Pixel owner would take for one that came with the phone, next to Recorder and Clock.

This sheet fixes the tokens and the rules. kit.css implements them and KIT.md lists every class, so the other sheets in this folder are built from what is shown here.

What is borrowed from the platform

  • Dynamic colour. The app has no brand colour. It takes the wallpaper scheme and uses the Material roles as named; a mock needs a concrete seed and this folder uses #3F6FD8.
  • The component set of Material 3 Expressive: short navigation bar with a pill indicator, pill search bar, connected button group, button groups whose members change width, icon buttons in narrow and wide, floating toolbar, hero and uncontained carousels, grouped lists with position-dependent corners (the Android 16 Settings look), bottom sheet with a drag handle, switch with icons, the morphing loading indicator.
  • From the Pixel media player: a scheme generated from the album art, the wavy seek track that flattens on pause, the play button that changes shape with state.
  • Google Sans Flex (OFL) with its weight, width and roundness axes, and Material Symbols Rounded (Apache 2.0).

What is Attic's own

  • The mark, recoloured from the scheme, and the wordmark set in the emphasised display style.
  • The shape assignments. Material supplies the shapes; which one means what is Attic's decision: the cookie for artists, a pill with a disc for instant mixes, a pill for Favourites on Home.
  • Home's structure: the pinned Favourites pill beside a hero carousel with one caption, mixes as buttons, songs in a group, the artist spotlight as a contained block.
  • Now Playing's layout: the pager with the next song showing, the three-button transport, the tools bar under it.
  • Titles that narrow before they truncate, using the width axis.
  • The names in the brief's shared table (Library, Favourites, Recap, Instant mixes).

The signature: playing has a shape

Attic shows play state with form, and it does so the same way everywhere. Playing is a squared-off play control and a wavy line. Paused is a round control and a flat line. The control is one component: 36dp inside a progress ring in the mini player, the compact header and the widget; 56dp beside a list that has its own play button; a wide 80dp button in Now Playing. A collection's labelled Play button follows the same rule and squares off when it reads Pause. The wave is the seek track, the mark on the current song in every list (over its cover, or in place of its number), and the progress line for long jobs. There is no accent colour reserved for "now playing", which is what lets the scheme stay the wallpaper's.

Colour rules

  • Screens are surface. Containers step up by role (surfaceContainer for a group row, surfaceContainerHigh for a pill or search bar). Surfaces are separated by tone. Only menus, snackbars, a dragged row and the FAB have a shadow.
  • primary is for the one filled action on a screen and for selected states. secondaryContainer is the navigation indicator, selected chips and the row that is playing. tertiaryContainer is Favourites.
  • Player surfaces add .from-art: mini player, Now Playing, queue, lyrics, collection headers, browse tiles, the widget. Nothing else takes colour from a cover, and no cover colour is used raw.
  • No gradients as decoration and no scrims over artwork. Text sits beside or under a cover, not on it.

Shape rules

  • Albums, playlists and folders are rounded squares; the radius follows the size (8 up to 48dp, 12 up to 64, 16 up to 128, 20 up to 180, 28 above).
  • Artists are the nine-lobed cookie at every size, from a 28dp byline to a 208dp header.
  • Buttons, bars and pills are fully round. A control that is on squares off: the play control while playing (36dp: 11 radius; 56dp: 18; wide: 28), a toggle that is selected (12), the cover in Now Playing against its paused form (28 against 48).
  • A group's outer corners are 20 and its inner corners 4, with 2dp between rows. A row lifted for dragging takes 20 on all corners.

Motion

WhatSpec
SchemeThe expressive motion scheme. Spatial springs (position, size, shape) overshoot: fast 0.6 damping / 800 stiffness, default 0.8 / 380, slow 0.8 / 200. Effects springs (colour, alpha) do not: fast 1.0 / 3800, default 1.0 / 1600, slow 1.0 / 800.
PressStock ripple, plus the button-group behaviour: the pressed button widens by 15% and its neighbours narrow, fast spatial. This replaces today's scale-down on press.
Play and pauseCorner radius and width morph on fast spatial; glyph cross-fades on fast effects. The same spring drives the Now Playing cover (scale 1 to 0.86, radius 28 to 48).
The waveSeek track: wavelength 32dp, amplitude 3.5dp, stroke 4dp, phase moving at one wavelength a second. Amplitude springs to 0 on pause and while scrubbing (default spatial). The list mark is the same wave at wavelength 10dp, stroke 2.4dp.
Toggle onContainer morphs round to rounded square on fast spatial; fill on fast effects. The heart scales 0.8 to 1 with the fast spatial overshoot.
Mini player to Now PlayingContainer transform. The cover is a shared element from 48dp to 302dp; the pill's bounds and corner radius animate to the full screen on default spatial; the remaining content fades on default effects.
Predictive backNow Playing, queue and lyrics scale down toward the mini player under the finger, and detail screens use the system's cross-activity style preview. Sheets and dialogs follow the system.
CarouselItems change width as they pass between the large, medium and small slots; the caption cross-fades when the large item changes.
App barThe root title scales from 36 to 22 as the page scrolls, and the bar's colour goes from surface to surfaceContainer on default effects.
LoadingThe loading indicator: a shape morphing through seven shapes while rotating, for waits under about five seconds. Longer jobs with a known length use linear progress.
Scheme changeWhen the song changes, every role of the art scheme animates on slow effects (about 600 ms, as today).

Open questions

  • Dynamic colour on, with what fallback? Pixel and most Android 12+ phones supply a wallpaper scheme. On a phone that does not, Attic needs one fixed scheme. This sheet's blue is a candidate; a seed taken from the mark is another.
  • The 2025 colour spec gives a bright tertiary container in dark theme (the Favourites pill). If that is too loud, the 2021 spec gives a dark one and everything else stays.
  • The cookie for artists. It is the least conventional choice here. It removes the circular artist image, which is a listed cue, and it is a shape Pixel already uses for contacts. The quieter alternative is a rounded square with a larger radius than albums.
  • Snackbar and text-field corners are 8 and 4, the Material defaults, next to much rounder surfaces. They can be raised if they look out of place on the later sheets.
