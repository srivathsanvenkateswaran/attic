06 · Queue and lyrics

2026-11-21 · UI directions · Pixel · sheet 06

The queue and the lyrics screen open from Now Playing, so they keep the scheme taken from the cover and follow the theme with it. Both are built from components the other sheets already use: grouped rows, the wave on the song that is playing, the play control that squares off, the wavy line for progress.

This sheet settles where “Clear” goes, how radio shows in the queue, and what replaces the white-sung, dark-upcoming lyric lines.

What was kept

  • Queue: “Now playing”, “Up next, {n} songs” and “Up next, 1 song”, “Nothing up next”, “Clear” only when something is up next.
  • Tap a row to jump to it, drag the handle to reorder, swipe either way to remove, long-press for the track menu. The current row keeps its menu button and does nothing on tap.
  • No history: songs already played are not listed. Shuffle shows as the real order.
  • Radio keeps ten songs up next, a swipe steers it, “Clear” stops it refilling.
  • Lyrics: auto-scroll with the current line about a third of the way down, tap a line to seek, “♪” for a blank timed line, plain text without highlighting, the close chevron, play and pause.
  • Every lyrics-card string: “Download once and they stay on this phone.”, “Download lyrics”, “This song is instrumental.”, “No lyrics were found for this song.”, and “Open lyrics” from the naming table.

Motion

  • Lyric lines. When the line changes, size and weight move on the default spatial spring and colour on the default effects spring, so the new line grows as the old one settles back. The list scrolls on the same spring.
  • Reorder. Picking a row up morphs its corners from 4 to 20 and raises it on the fast spatial spring; the rows it passes slide by one place.
  • Swipe to remove. The row follows the finger. Past half its width the bin scales up once (fast spatial), and on release the group closes over the gap.
  • Opening. Both screens rise from the control that opened them: the queue from the queue button in the tools bar, lyrics from the lyrics card, as container transforms.

Open questions

  • The note under a radio queue is new text: “Radio keeps ten songs up next and adds more as these play. Swipe one away to hear fewer like it. Clear stops radio adding to this queue.” It states what the app already does. It can be cut to the first sentence, or dropped.
  • The bottom bar on the lyrics screen shows progress but does not seek. Today the screen has a play button and nothing else, and seeking is by tapping a line. Making the line draggable would add a control this screen does not have.
  • “Nothing up next” has no second line. With Autoplay on, radio continues when the last song ends, so a line saying that would be true. It is not written today, so it is not drawn.
  • Lyric line colours differ from the kit's. The kit's .lyrics has the current line in onSurface and upcoming lines in outline, which the lyrics card uses. This screen uses primary for the current line to match the lyric line above the title in Now Playing. One of the two should move.
