Now Playing

2026-11-21 · UI directions · apple · sheet 05

The player is the one screen where the chrome gives up its own colour. The background is the cover's own gradients, enlarged, blurred and dimmed with the cover's darkest shade, and every control on it is white. Play, previous and next are bare glyphs. The seek bar is a thick bar with no handle. Shuffle, queue and repeat sit in a second row, and a toggle that is on becomes a white lozenge with the glyph knocked out.

The same field, at lower strength, is what lights the mini player on every other screen.

Kept

  • Everything in the Now Playing and mini player parts of the contract: context name, close and swipe-down, menu, cover you can swipe sideways, artist tap-through, seek by tap and drag with elapsed and total, shuffle, repeat off / all / one, queue, the inline lyric line, the three cards, the compact header.
  • No volume slider, no output picker, no time-remaining display. The row under the transport has only controls that exist.
  • Every button is at least 48dp in both directions. Transport targets are 76dp.

Open questions

  • Light-theme player (frame D). Today Now Playing, Lyrics and the mini player ignore the theme. Here the field follows it. If you prefer the dark field in both themes, add deep to the frame and D becomes A; nothing else changes.
  • Queue in the middle. Shuffle and repeat sit at the two ends where they are today, with queue between them. The alternative is shuffle and repeat together on the left and queue alone on the right.
  • Radio. As today, the only sign of radio on this screen is the context line. A radio glyph before the context name would cost nothing; it is not drawn because the contract lists no radio indicator.
