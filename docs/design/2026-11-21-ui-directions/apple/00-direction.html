Direction: Attic in the Apple idiom

2026-11-21 · UI directions · apple · sheet 00

This folder takes the grammar of Apple's Music app and the iOS design language around it and writes Attic in it: large titles that collapse, lists divided by hairlines that start at the text, glass bars that float over the content, and a player coloured by its artwork. It runs on Android, so the status bar, the gesture pill, system back, the notification and the widget are Android's.

It is not Apple Music with the logo changed. The typeface is Instrument Sans, the accent is a warm orange called Ember on warm near-black and paper white, the icons are drawn for this kit, and Home, the mini player and the player's control rows are arranged differently from Apple's. The one thing to remember it by is lamplight: the song that is playing is the only thing that colours the chrome. Its artwork fills the player, leaks into the mini player's glass from the cover's side, and fills the widget. Everything that is not playing stays Soot, Paper and Ember.

Buttons and controls

Borrowed from the platform

  • Large titles that collapse. Root screens open on a 34sp bold title. On scroll it becomes a 17sp centred title over a blurred edge.
  • Lists, not cards. Rows are divided by a hairline that starts at the text edge. Boxes appear only where iOS uses them: grouped Settings lists and the panels under the player.
  • Glass. The tab bar, mini player, round buttons, sheets, menus, alerts and toasts are translucent and blurred, float clear of the screen edges, and always have content behind them.
  • Artwork as the colour. The player's background is made from the cover. Collection pages get the cover's light under the cover only.
  • Controls. Bare transport glyphs, a thick seek bar without a handle, two equal buttons for play and shuffle, menus with the icon at the trailing edge, a check for the chosen option, segmented controls.

Attic's own

  • Ember and warm neutrals. Not Apple Music's red-pink, not the green in use today. The accent appears only on things that respond to a tap.
  • Instrument Sans in place of a system face, for its narrower set and firm bold.
  • The icon set, including the Attic house as the Home tab. The mark is otherwise used as a mark: permission screen, Recap cards, widget, launcher.
  • Home. Covers for shortcuts, paged rows for instant mixes, a list for folders. Apple's Home is a column of large editorial cards; today's is tiles and shelves. This is neither.
  • The mini player. One capsule lit by the artwork, with a progress ring. Apple's shows no progress; today's draws a line along the bottom.
  • The player's second row. Shuffle, queue, repeat, with a lozenge for "on". There is no volume slider or output button because Attic has neither.
  • A light-theme player built from the cover's pale shade. Apple keeps its player dark.
  • Android behaviour. System back closes Lyrics, then Queue, then the player. Sheets are attached to the bottom edge. The notification, lock screen and widget are drawn by Android's rules in sheet 10.

The signature: lamplight

An attic is dark until something is switched on. The chrome here is two neutrals and one accent, and the only other colour allowed into it is the artwork of the song that is playing. It fills a whole surface (.lit: player, lyrics, widget, Recap card), falls as light under a cover (.art.glow: collection headers), or blooms inside glass (.bloom: mini player). A screen with nothing playing has no colour but Ember and the covers themselves.

Rules the other sheets should hold to

  • Glass needs something behind it. Do not place a glass bar over an empty background.
  • One filled Ember button per screen at most. Its partner is tinted.
  • No heading in capitals, no label above a heading. A heading may have one line of explanation under it.
  • No gradient that is not made from a cover.
  • The title of the playing song is never coloured. The bars are the mark.

Open questions

  • Ember. It sits between orange and red. If it reads too close to another product you know, the accent is --accent and --accent-wash in each theme, and nothing else depends on its hue.
  • Player in light theme. See sheet 05, frame D.
  • Typeface weight. Instrument Sans stops at Bold. If you want heavier titles than this specimen shows, the family has to change, not the weight.
