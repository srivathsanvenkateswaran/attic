Home

2026-11-21 · UI directions · apple · sheet 01

Home keeps every block it has today and changes the form of each one. The page opens on a large title and a four-way segmented filter. The six shortcuts are covers in a 3 × 2 grid. Instant mixes are song rows in columns that page sideways. Folders are a plain list with chevrons. Only albums, recent songs and artists stay as horizontal shelves, so no two neighbouring sections share a shape.

Ember marks the things you can press. The only other colour in the chrome is the artwork of the song that is playing, which lights the mini player's glass from its leading edge.

Kept

  • Every section, its order, its contents and its limits: six shortcuts with Favourites first, the Recap entry, nine instant mixes, twelve recent songs, four random songs, folders, the 14 biggest albums, the artist spotlight, the 14 biggest artists.
  • The four filters and what each leaves on the page. Exactly one is always selected.
  • Shelves, for recent songs, albums, the artist spotlight and top artists. A row of covers under a bold heading is on the list of Spotify cues, and it is also how every large music app shows a set too long to list. Here it is one shape among several on the page, and no two shelves share a cover size.
  • Tap targets: every row is at least 56dp tall, the segmented control and small buttons take the missing height as invisible slop.
  • Tap on an instant mix starts radio, long-press opens the track menu. Tap on a recent song plays the recents list from it.

Open questions

  • Settings as a gear. The Attic mark now appears in the app only as the Home tab icon (and on the permission screen, Recap cards, widget and launcher). If you want the mark on the title row, it can replace the gear, but it then reads as a logo and not as a button.
  • Instant mixes as rows. Rows show three songs per column and need a swipe to reach the other six. If nine at a glance matters more than seeing the artist, the alternative is a 3 × 3 cover grid, which would then need the shortcuts to change shape.
  • "Albums" as a grid. Frame C changes the layout when a filter is on, not only the contents. The same would apply to "Artists" (a grid of circles) and "Folders" (the list from frame B).
