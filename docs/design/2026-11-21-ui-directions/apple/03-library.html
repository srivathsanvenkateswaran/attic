Kept

  • The unfiltered list and its order: Favourites, your playlists, folders, the 40 most recently played artists, every album. Albums and artists are ordered by how recently one of their songs was played, then by name.
  • What each filter holds. Playlists: Favourites, your playlists, folders. Albums: every album. Artists: every artist. Songs: every song.
  • List view by default; the three-column grid with round, centred artist tiles and two-line names.
  • On Songs: the three orders, the label format "{order}, {n} songs", tap a row to play the whole list from there with the context "All songs", and no rows-or-grid switch.
  • The dialog: title, a field labelled "Playlist name" with focus, "Cancel", and "Create" disabled while the field is blank.
  • Settings is reachable from this tab root.

Where this differs from Apple's Music app

  • Apple's Library is the same idea of a category list above a grid, and this screen is the closest in the set to an Apple screen. The differences: each row carries a count; the section under the list is everything you can open in "Recents" order with Favourites pinned first, not recently added albums; it defaults to rows and has a rows-or-grid switch; the grid is three columns with circles for artists.
  • Apple puts create-playlist inside the Playlists page. Here it is also in the title row, beside a search shortcut and Settings.
  • Apple's song list opens with two large buttons, Play and Shuffle, and keeps its sort options in a menu. Here the sort is the visible label, and there is one Shuffle capsule because Attic has no "play all" on this list today.
  • Apple's app has nothing like a Music/Offline folder shown as a read-only playlist. Here folders are a labelled group inside Playlists and carry their own glyph elsewhere.

Open questions

  • Rows that open pages, or chips that filter in place. The kit also has chips (sheet 00). Chips keep you on one screen and toggle with one tap; rows give each list a title, a count and room for its own controls, and cost a back gesture to leave. This sheet draws rows. If you want chips, frames C, E and F become filtered states of frame A and nothing else changes.
  • Four rows or six. Favourites and Folders could be rows of their own. They are left inside Playlists so the four rows map one to one onto today's four chips, and Favourites is already the first item under "Recents".
  • The word "Recents". It is kept as a heading because the shared naming table does not replace it. The list under it starts with Favourites, playlists and folders in a fixed order before anything recent, which is today's behaviour. "Everything" would describe the list more truthfully.
  • Sort as a menu. Frame I replaces the tap-to-cycle label with a menu. If you prefer the cycle, the label stays exactly as drawn in frame C and the menu is dropped.
  • Three buttons in the title row. Search, plus and Settings. Search could go, since the Search tab is one tap away in the dock; it is kept because the feature list names it.
