Search opens on a large title, a field, and "Browse": a tile for every folder and for the eight biggest albums, each coloured by its own artwork. Typing replaces the tiles with results as you type. Results split by what a tap does. Artists, folders and albums open a page, so they are covers in one row. Songs play, so they are the list.

The only colour on the empty screen is the tiles, and all of it comes from covers.

Kept

  • Results update on every keystroke. There is no submit step, no history and no suggestions.
  • What is searched and how it ranks: songs, artists, albums and folders, ignoring case and accents; names that start with the query first, then names that contain it, then songs matched through their artist or album. User playlists are not searched.
  • The limits: up to five artists, every matching folder, up to ten albums, every matching song.
  • The type labels "Artist", "Playlist" and "Album, {artist}".
  • Tapping a song plays every matching song from that row, with the context "Search results". Long-press or the three dots opens the track menu.
  • Browse holds every folder, then the eight albums with the most songs. A tap opens the folder or album.
  • The clear button appears once there is text and empties the field.
  • No results for "{query}" and "Check the spelling, or try fewer words."

Where this differs from Apple's Music app

  • Apple's search has a scope switch between the catalogue and the library, recent searches, and a Cancel button beside the field. Attic has one scope and none of the rest, so the field is the only control.
  • Apple's browse tiles are editorial categories with their own artwork. These are the folders and albums on the phone, and each takes its whole field from its cover, the same mechanism as the player and the Recap card.
  • Apple shows results as one mixed column ordered by relevance. Here the kinds are in a fixed order and songs have their own counted group, because the app ranks by name match and has no relevance score to sort a mixed list by.

Open questions

  • Order of results. Today's order is artists, folders, songs, albums. This moves albums up to join the other things a tap opens. With a one-letter query that can put up to five artists, every folder and ten albums ahead of the first song. If that is too many, the albums can go back under the songs with the same taller row.
  • Browse under "No results". Frame C shows the tiles below the message. Today the screen is empty there. If you prefer the empty screen, the message centres and the dock sits over a blank page.
  • Frame B and the brief. The brief asks for "dhur" to return an artist, albums, a playlist and songs. With the fixture library it returns two albums and ten songs, which is what frame B draws. Frame E ("ph") carries the four-type case.
  • Keyboard. The frames show the screen with the keyboard put away so the results and the dock are visible. With it up, the dock is hidden and the list ends at the keyboard, as today.
