Both screens open from Now Playing and stay on its artwork field, so going from the player to the queue or the lyrics changes what is written on the surface and not the surface. Each closes with the same pull-down glyph, in the same place, that closes the player. System back does the same: Lyrics, then Queue, then the player.

The queue is a titled list with "Clear" on the line it clears. Lyrics are the one place the type goes to 30sp bold: the line being sung is at full strength, sung lines stay sharp and dim, and the lines still to come lose focus the further away they are.

Kept

  • Queue. "Now playing" and "Up next, {n} songs" / "Up next, 1 song" / "Nothing up next". Tap a row to jump to it. Drag the handle to reorder. Swipe either way to remove. Long-press a row for the track menu; the playing row keeps its menu button and does nothing on tap. "Clear" only when something is up next. Played songs are not listed. Shuffle shows as the real order.
  • Radio. Ten songs kept up next, added at the end. A swipe steers it. "Clear" stops it refilling.
  • Lyrics. Synced lines that follow playback and scroll so the current line sits about a third of the way down, seek on tap, "♪" for a blank timed line, plain text without highlighting, a download button when there is nothing, play and pause. No seek bar and no skip buttons, as today.
  • Card. All six states with today's sentences. The busy state swaps the button for a spinner.

Where this differs from Apple Music

  • Apple keeps the queue inside the player with the transport under it and shuffle, repeat and autoplay buttons beside the heading. Here the queue is its own layer with a large title, no transport, and "Clear", which Apple does not have.
  • Apple's lyrics keep a header with the cover and bring the full transport up on a tap. Here the top holds only the close glyph and the bottom holds one glass capsule with a progress ring.
  • Apple fills each line word by word. Attic's lyrics are timed per line, so a whole line changes at once.
  • Apple's player and lyrics stay dark in light mode. Here they follow the theme (frames F and I).

Open questions

  • Queue on the field. KIT.md describes the queue on plain Soot or Paper; this sheet follows the later direction note and puts it on the artwork. Dropping lit art-… from the frame returns it to the plain surface with nothing else to change. On the field the swipe capsule and the lifted row need the two overrides in this sheet's style block.
  • New copy. "Radio keeps ten songs lined up. Swipe one away and it plays fewer like it." (B), "Use Play next or Add to queue on any song." (C), and the reuse of "Download once and they stay on this phone." on the lyrics screen (H). Strike any of them and the layout holds.
  • The capsule on the lyrics screen. It has no heart, because today's lyrics screen has none. Adding it would make the capsule identical to the mini player. Tapping the capsule body could close lyrics and return to the player; that is not drawn as a promise.
  • Pause in the queue. Today the queue has no transport and this keeps it that way. The same capsule could sit at the bottom of the queue if you want pause there.
