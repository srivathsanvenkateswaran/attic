Outside the app: widget, notifications, launcher icon

2026-11-21 · UI directions · apple · sheet 10

These are the places where Attic appears on surfaces Android owns. The widget is the only one Attic draws in full, and it carries the direction's signature: the artwork of the current song as a blurred field, made by the app as a bitmap because a widget cannot blur what is behind it. The media notification and the lock-screen card are drawn by Android from what the app hands over, so they are shown as Android draws them, in its typeface and its colours, with only the two custom action glyphs changed.

The mark is being redesigned separately. Every mark here is the current house outline as a stand-in; what this sheet proposes is where the mark goes and what it sits on.

D · Launcher icon, colour and themed

An adaptive icon. The background layer is flat Ember and the glyph is the kit's dark ink for Ember, the pair the app uses for its one filled button. It is neither a bright glyph on black, as today, nor a white glyph on a gradient. The themed icon is the same glyph as a monochrome layer, which Android colours from the wallpaper in dark and light. The house is the current mark standing in.

Dashed square: the 72dp a mask can show of the 108dp canvas. Dashed circle: the 66dp safe zone every mask keeps. The stand-in fits inside it with room to spare, so a replacement mark drawn to the same 42dp box will too.

How the widget can be built

It stays one AppWidgetProvider with three RemoteViews layouts chosen by size, as today. No part of it needs live blur, which a widget cannot have.

  • The field is an ImageView behind everything, filled with a bitmap the app makes when the song changes: the cover scaled down to about 32px and back up (which blurs it), then the cover's dark shade drawn over it at 57 to 80 per cent. It replaces the single colour the widget is filled with today. The corners come from clipToOutline with the system widget radius on Android 12 and later, and from a rounded bitmap before that.
  • The small size uses the same step to bake one bitmap: the cover, with its lower third blurred and darkened.
  • Controls are ImageButtons with the kit's glyphs as vector drawables and a 48dp box each.
  • Album tiles are five ImageViews with rounded bitmaps and a pending intent each, as today.
  • Type. The mock sets the title in Instrument Sans through android:fontFamily in the layout. Today's widget uses the system font. See the open question.
  • No progress bar, no heart and no shuffle on the widget; it has none today.

Kept

  • One widget in three layouts chosen by size: small with cover and play or pause; wide with cover, title, artist, previous, play or pause, next; large with the same, the Attic mark, and the five album shortcuts.
  • Tapping the widget anywhere else opens the app. The idle text, the kept last song after a reboot, the mark in the art slot when a song has no cover, and the four button descriptions.
  • The media notification's contents and its two extra actions; it appears when playback starts.
  • The analysis notification: silent, ongoing, title, "84 of 127", determinate progress, "Stop", tap to open the app.
  • An adaptive launcher icon with a monochrome layer for themed icons.

Open questions

  • The mark. Every mark on this sheet is the current house outline standing in. The proposal is its container: flat Ember under dark ink for the launcher, white for the status bar and notification, the widget corner and the art-slot fallback. A new glyph drops into the same 42dp box.
  • Ember as a whole surface. Sheet 00 says Ember is never the background of a surface. The launcher icon breaks that on purpose, because it is the one thing Attic owns that is itself a button. The alternative that keeps the rule is a Paper icon with a Soot glyph, which is quieter on a home screen.
  • Widget typeface. The mock assumes a font resource named in the widget's layout XML is used by the launcher, which Android has allowed since version 8. It has not been tried on a device for this round. If a launcher ignores it, the widget shows the system font, as today, and nothing else changes.
  • Widget in light theme. The field is dark on every wallpaper and in both themes, as the kit prescribes for the widget. A pale field that follows the system theme is possible with a second bitmap.
  • Colour on the media card. Current Android versions colour that card from the artwork and ignore the app's colour hint, so it will not show Ember. Nothing to decide; it is noted so the difference from the analysis notification is not read as an oversight.
