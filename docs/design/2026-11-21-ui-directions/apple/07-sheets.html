Sheets, menus, dialogs and toasts

2026-11-21 · UI directions · apple · sheet 07

Today every list of choices is a Material bottom sheet. Here the form follows the length of the list. Actions on one thing (a song, a playlist) are a glass menu that opens next to the thing. Lists that can grow or that you search (playlists, languages, every song in the library) stay sheets attached to the bottom edge. Questions are centred alerts, and confirmations are a toast.

Each is thick glass with the screen visibly behind it. Every action and message is the one the app has today, with the shared renames.

Which is a menu and which is a sheet

  • Menu: track menu (8 or 9 actions), playlist options (2). Fixed lists of verbs about the thing under your finger. They close on a tap outside or on system back.
  • Sheet: "Add to playlist" (grows with your playlists and starts a create flow), "Song language" (seven choices and a sentence of explanation), "Add songs" (the whole library, searchable, stays open). They close on swipe down, a tap on the scrim, or system back.
  • "Add to playlist" and "Language" in the track menu close the menu and raise the sheet, as the track sheet is replaced today.

Kept

  • Every track action with its condition: "Start radio", "Add to favourites" / "Remove from favourites", "Play next", "Add to queue", "Add to playlist", "Language", "Remove from this playlist" (playlist rows only), "Go to album" and "Go to artist" (only when they are in the library).
  • The menu opens from a row's menu button, a long-press on a row, an Instant mixes row, a Recap top song, a queue row, and the player.
  • Picker: "New playlist" first, then user playlists only; tap adds, closes and reports "Added to …" or "Already in …". Opens for one song or a whole collection.
  • Language choices in today's order: Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, English, No vocals. Tap saves and closes; dismissing changes nothing.
  • Add songs: placeholder "Find a song", filters by title and artist, tap on the row or the button toggles at once, the sheet stays open.
  • Name dialog titles "Name this playlist", "New playlist", "Rename playlist"; label "Playlist name"; confirm "Create" or "Rename", disabled while blank. Delete dialog title, body and both button labels.
  • All eleven messages from section 2.17. Favouriting, removing from a playlist, clearing the queue, renaming and deleting still show none.

Where this differs from Apple Music

  • Apple pushes its playlist picker as a full-height page with a search field and nests choices in submenus. Here pickers are bottom sheets sized to their content, which suits a tall Android phone and closes on back.
  • Apple's alerts are centred text over two text buttons split by hairlines. These are left-aligned with capsule buttons.
  • Apple confirms "Play next" with a square panel in the middle of the screen. This is a one-line pill above the mini player.
  • Apple's song menu starts with library and download actions. This one starts with "Start radio", which is what Attic does that a file player does not.

Open questions

  • Menu or sheet for the track menu. A 9-row menu is 456dp tall. It fits beside any row on this frame, opening upward in the lower half of a list, but it is the tallest menu the language would normally carry. The kit's .sheet with .sheet-head draws the same rows as a bottom sheet if you prefer that; the direction sheet shows it.
  • Group order. Play, keep, go. If "Add to favourites" is your most-used row it could lead.
  • "Done" in Add songs. New control, though it only closes the sheet. Remove it and the title row is just the title.
  • New copy. "Radio uses the language to choose what plays next, and Recap counts it." in the language sheet, and the line under each sheet title naming the song or playlist. Both can go without moving anything else.
  • Toast shape. A pill reads well for two or three words and less well for the two-line radio message (frame J). The alternative is a 22dp-radius rectangle for every message.
