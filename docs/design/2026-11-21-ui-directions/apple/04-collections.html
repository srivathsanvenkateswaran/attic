Collections: album, artist, playlist, Favourites, folder

2026-11-21 · UI directions · apple · sheet 04

The five collection screens share one frame. A centred cover lights the page under it, the title and one or two lines of detail sit below, then two equal buttons for Play and Shuffle, then the songs with hairlines from the text edge. What you can do to the collection as a whole lives in the top bar, on glass.

Each kind changes one thing. An album numbers its rows and links to its artist. An artist shows records and a large name. A playlist you made adds a line for "Add songs" and "Reorder". Favourites swaps the cover for the Ember tile. A folder says where it comes from.

Kept

  • Play starts from the first song with shuffle off, or toggles play and pause when this collection is the one playing. Shuffle starts it shuffled from a random song.
  • Add the whole collection to a playlist, on every collection, disabled when it is empty.
  • Album: the artist link, "Album" or "Single" and the year, tracks in track order without covers, the footer count and duration.
  • Artist: "{n} songs, {duration}", the first five songs with ranks, the albums, "All songs" when there are more than five. Playing any song queues the artist's whole list.
  • Folder: "Playlist from your Music/Offline folder", the count and duration, and no editing.
  • Favourites: newest first, and its empty state with the buttons still shown.
  • Playlist: add songs, reorder by dragging a handle, rename and delete from "Playlist options" (sheet 07 draws the menu and the dialogs), "Remove from this playlist" in the track menu, and the empty state.
  • Every row: tap plays in context, long-press or the three dots opens the track menu. In reorder mode a tap does not play and long-press still opens the menu.
  • The "is gone" state is on sheet 08.

Where this differs from Apple's Music app

  • Apple's artist page opens on a full-bleed photograph with the name over it. Attic has no photographs, so the header is built from covers and says so by keeping them square.
  • Apple's two buttons are the same grey fill with tinted labels. Here Play is filled and Shuffle is tinted, so the pair has a primary and the fill can move to Shuffle to show that shuffle is on.
  • Apple's album page has controls for adding to the library, downloading and favouriting. Attic has none of those for an album; the bar holds back and add-to-playlist only.
  • Apple edits a playlist in a separate editing mode opened from its "more" menu. Here reordering happens in place and "Add songs" is always on the page.
  • Apple's album pages go on to shelves of more music by the artist. An album here ends at its footer.

Open questions

  • The artist header. Three sleeves for an artist with several albums, one for an artist with one. Most names in this library are credited on a single album, so the one-sleeve header will be the common case, and it resembles an album page with a larger title and no artist line. The plainer alternative is no picture at all: the name as a left-aligned large title, as on Home and Library.
  • Circles elsewhere. Lists, grids and search results still show an artist as a round crop of a cover, because the kit uses the circle to mean "artist". If the header's rule should apply everywhere, artists in lists become small square sleeves and lose that signal.
  • Where add-to-playlist lives. Sheet 00 draws it as a third, grey button beside Play and Shuffle. This sheet moves it to the top bar so the two main buttons are equal in width. Either works with the kit as it is.
  • Short albums and the bar title. In frame F the bar has no title because the page's title is still visible. If you would rather the bar always names the page once the cover has passed, the title will show twice on short albums.
  • "0 songs, 0 min". Kept from today on empty collections. "No songs yet" would read better.
