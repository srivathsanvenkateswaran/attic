How a card gets its colour

A card about one thing is lit by that thing's cover: top song and top songs by the first song, top artist and top artists by the first artist's cover. A card about no single thing takes the top covers in rank order: intro the first, minutes the second, languages the third, the clock the fourth, highlights the fifth, and the summary returns to the first. With fewer than five top songs the sequence wraps. A song without artwork gives a Soot card with white type. The field is the cover scaled up and blurred, under a veil of its darkest shade at 57 to 80 per cent, which is the recipe the player uses. Sheet 00 measured that recipe on eight covers and found secondary text above 4:1 on each.

Kept

  • The four periods, with "This year" as the default, and the period wording table: headline, card-footer label and intro line for each.
  • All six figures and their labels, up to ten top songs with play counts and play-all, up to ten top artists with minutes, the language breakdown, the 24-hour clock with listener type and busiest hour, the log-start footnote.
  • Tap a top song to play the list from it and long-press for the track menu; tap an artist to open the artist.
  • "Share a card": thumbnails of the same cards, each opening the story at that card.
  • All ten card types, in the same order, each skipped under the same conditions, on the same 360 × 640dp canvas, exported as a 1080 × 1920 PNG with the same file names. The image holds the card only.
  • Seven seconds a card, tap the left third to go back, tap elsewhere to go on, press and hold to pause, pause while saving or sharing, stay on the last card.
  • "Save" to Pictures/Attic with its two messages, "Share" to the system sheet.
  • The mini player and tab bar stay on the page and are absent from the story.
  • The Home card's conditions and its switch of headline on 24 December.
  • Loading is the kit's centred spinner under the period control. The empty period is frame G.

Open questions

  • Listener type. "Night owl" and the other three labels are on the list of Spotify cues. They are kept here as a plain heading under the clock, with no persona card of their own. Dropping the label and keeping only its sentence ("Most of your listening happens after dark.") would remove the cue completely.
  • Summary without the names. Songs two to five appear on the summary as covers, and artists two to five do not appear. If the shared image must name all ten, the two columns come back and so does the resemblance.
  • Dots in place of segments. The contract says "progress segments". Ten dots do the same job. If a period yields fewer cards the row shortens. If you would rather have no timer at all, the cards could simply be swiped, but that changes behaviour and is not drawn.
  • Story always dark. The story and the cards ignore the light theme, as today, because the card is an image that has to look the same wherever it is sent.
  • Colour from artwork can be dull. A listener whose top covers are all grey gets grey cards. The poster colours never had that problem. A floor on saturation is possible; it is not drawn.
  • The 84sp numeral is the largest type in the app. It holds six digits at that size; a seven-digit count in "All time" would have to drop to 76sp. On the Minutes card the count is 100sp up to five digits and 82sp for six, the way today's card steps from 116 to 92.
