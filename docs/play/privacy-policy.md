# Attic privacy policy

Effective [date of publication]. Attic is a free, open-source music player for Android, published by [developer name as shown on Google Play]. Its source code is at https://github.com/srivathsanvenkateswaran/attic.

## What Attic sends off your phone

Attic has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. The developer runs no server and receives no data from the app.

The only thing Attic sends over the internet is a lyrics lookup, and only when you ask for one by tapping Download on a song's lyrics or "Download lyrics for all songs" in Settings. Each lookup sends the song's title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB (https://lrclib.net), a free lyrics service run by a third party, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The request also carries Attic's name and version. LRCLIB, like any web server, sees your IP address. Your music files are never uploaded. LRCLIB's handling of these requests is governed by LRCLIB, not by this policy.

## What stays on your phone

Attic reads the audio files on your phone to play them, with the Music and audio permission you grant. Likes, playlists, play history, the saved queue, downloaded lyrics and the results of song analysis are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Song analysis runs entirely on the phone. Uninstalling Attic or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

Notifications show playback controls and the progress of song analysis. They are not used for anything else.

## Children

Attic is not directed at children under 13.
